BRISBANE · WALKING ROUTE

Brisbane walking route: South Bank and river

Distance 2.494 kmWalk 31 minStops 5Toilet / water 3

This walking route takes you along the Brisbane River, starting at the Queensland Performing Arts Centre and ending at the historic Brisbane City Hall. It’s a fantastic way to experience the heart of South Bank and enjoy the riverside views.

Begin your journey at the Queensland Performing Arts Centre. From here, head towards the South Bank Parklands, a beautiful green space. The walk to the parklands is approximately 6 minutes and 28 seconds, covering a distance of 0.556 km. Once you’ve enjoyed the park, continue onward towards the Queensland Museum.

Next, you’ll walk approximately 9 minutes and 41 seconds, covering 0.776 km, to reach the Queensland Museum. From there, it’s a short 4 minute and 58 second walk – 0.425 km – to Victoria Bridge. Cross the bridge and then continue your walk for another 9 minutes and 47 seconds, covering 0.737 km, to reach the impressive Brisbane City Hall.
