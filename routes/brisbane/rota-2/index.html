BRISBANE · WALKING ROUTE

Brisbane walking route: City Botanic Gardens and central Brisbane

Distance 3.182 kmWalk 42 minStops 5Toilet / water 6

This walking route will take you through some of Brisbane’s most beautiful green spaces and bustling city centres. Let’s begin at the City Botanic Gardens, a wonderful place to start your exploration. From here, you’ll head towards Parliament House, a significant landmark.

Your first leg of the journey is a short 0.535 km walk, taking approximately 6 minutes and 29 seconds. Once you arrive at Parliament House, continue your walk towards the vibrant Queen Street Mall. This section is a little longer, covering 0.864 km and taking around 11 minutes and 45 seconds.

Next, you'll move onto the Queen Street Mall, a central plaza. From there, it’s a quick 0.47 km walk to ANZAC Square, another popular public space. This leg of the journey will take you about 6 minutes and 14 seconds.
