berlin walking route: Mitte anıtları · Laloo

2.231 km 30 min walk 4 stops 0 toilet / water

This route takes you to explore some significant monuments in the Mitte district of Berlin. It’s a manageable walk, perfect for a couple of hours of exploration. We’ll start at the iconic Brandenburg Gate and work our way towards Potsdamer Platz.

Begin your journey at the Brandenburg Gate. From here, head north-east for approximately 8 minutes and 45 seconds. You’ll cover a distance of 0.617 km, arriving at the Reichstag Building. This historic building is a key landmark and a fantastic starting point for your walk.

Next, walk from the Reichstag Building for around 13 minutes and 2 seconds. This leg of the journey is 0.949 km long. You’ll find yourself at the Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe, a profoundly moving and important site.

Finally, complete the route by walking for approximately 8 minutes and 28 seconds – a distance of 0.665 km – to reach Potsdamer Platz. This vibrant square is a central hub and a fitting end to your exploration of these Mitte monuments.

The total distance of this walking route is 2.231 km and it should take around 30 minutes and 16 seconds to complete. Please note that restrooms and water sources are not recorded along this route.
