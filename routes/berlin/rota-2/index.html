berlin walking route: Müze Adası ve Alexanderplatz · Laloo

2.464 km 31 min walk 4 stops 1 toilet / water

This walking route offers a fantastic way to experience a key part of Berlin, beginning with the impressive Berlin Cathedral. From here, you’ll embark on a short journey of approximately 3 minutes and 31 seconds, covering a distance of 0.314 kilometers, to reach the renowned Museum Island.

Once at Museum Island, you’ll spend some time exploring this significant cultural hub. From Museum Island, continue your walk for around 19 minutes and 5 seconds, covering a distance of 1.454 kilometers. This will take you to the bustling Alexanderplatz.

Your final destination is the Rotes Rathaus, located just 8 minutes and 28 seconds away. This leg of your journey is only 0.696 kilometers long.

The entire route covers a total distance of 2.464 kilometers and will take approximately 31 minutes and 3 seconds to complete. A public restroom is available about 111 meters off the route.
