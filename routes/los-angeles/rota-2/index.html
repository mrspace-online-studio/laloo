Los-angeles walking route: Hollywood bulvarı

2.314 km 27 min walk 4 stops 2 toilet / water

This route takes you past some of Hollywood’s most famous theatres, offering a fantastic glimpse into the heart of the entertainment district. The walk is relatively short, taking approximately 27 minutes to complete.

Begin your journey at the TCL Chinese Theatre (anit). From here, head east along Hollywood Boulevard for approximately 0.322 km. This short walk will take you around 3 minutes and 58 seconds. You’ll arrive at the impressive Dolby Theatre (anit), a significant landmark.

Next, continue your walk from the Dolby Theatre to the El Capitan Theatre (anit). This stretch is a bit longer, covering 0.512 km and taking around 6 minutes. Then, head west on Hollywood Boulevard for 1.48 km, a journey that should take you around 17 minutes and 29 seconds.

Finally, you’ll reach the Hollywood Pantages Theatre (anit) marking the end of your walking tour.

During your walk, you can find refreshments at the nearby Starbucks (cafe) and The Pie Hole (cafe), both located just a short distance off the route.

Total distance of this walking route is 2.314 km.
