LOS ANGELES · WALKING ROUTE

Los Angeles walking route: Hollywood boulevard

Distance 2.314 kmWalk 27 minStops 4Toilet / water 1

Hey! So you want to check out a little bit of Hollywood Boulevard, huh? It’s a fun walk, really easy to do. You’ll start at the TCL Chinese Theatre, a pretty iconic spot. Then it's just a short stroll, about 5 minutes, to the Dolby Theatre.

From there, you'll keep going until you reach the El Capitan Theatre – that's another quick 5 minutes. This part is nice and flat, so it's easy walking. There’s a water point along this leg if you need to top up.

The last stretch to the Hollywood Pantages Theatre is a bit longer, about 15 minutes, but still pretty manageable. There's a public restroom or drinking water point somewhere nearby the route, so you're covered. The whole walk is about 25 minutes and just over 2 kilometers.

Definitely wear comfy shoes, and grab some water before you go. Enjoy the people-watching!
