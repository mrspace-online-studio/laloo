LOS ANGELES · WALKING ROUTE

Los Angeles walking route: Santa Monica center

Distance 3.023 kmWalk 38 minStops 4Toilet / water 3

This route takes you through the heart of Santa Monica, starting at the iconic Santa Monica Pier and ending at the beautiful Palisades Park. It’s a fantastic way to experience the city’s vibrant atmosphere.

Begin your journey at the Santa Monica Pier, a historic landmark. From here, head towards Tongva Park. The walk is roughly 0.6 kilometres and should take around 9 minutes. This park offers a peaceful space to relax and enjoy the coastal views.

Next, you’ll make your way to Third Street Promenade, a bustling pedestrian shopping area. This section of the walk is approximately 1 kilometre and will take roughly 13 minutes. You’ll find plenty of shops and restaurants here.
