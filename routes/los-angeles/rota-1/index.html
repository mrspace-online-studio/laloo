Los-angeles walking route: Downtown merkez

2.086 km 26 min walk 4 stops 0 toilet / water

This route takes you through some of Downtown Los Angeles’s most iconic landmarks, starting at Union Station and ending at the Walt Disney Concert Hall. It’s a fantastic way to experience the heart of the city!

Begin your journey at Union Station, a significant landmark. From here, head south along a clearly marked path. The walk to Olvera Street is a short one, covering approximately 0.57 kilometers and taking around 6 minutes and 52 seconds. You’ll arrive at Olvera Street, a vibrant public space.

Next, continue your walk to Grand Park. This leg of the route is a little longer, covering 1.035 kilometers and taking roughly 13 minutes and 1 second. You’ll find yourself in Grand Park, a beautiful green space.

Finally, your last leg takes you from Grand Park to the Walt Disney Concert Hall. This is a quick 0.481 kilometer walk, which should take about 6 minutes and 31 seconds.

The total walking distance for this route is 2.086 kilometers, and it will take you approximately 26 minutes and 25 seconds to complete.

Restrooms and water are not available along this route.
