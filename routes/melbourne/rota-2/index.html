# Melbourne Walking Route: Docklands & Flagstaff

This route takes you through some key areas of Melbourne, starting at the bustling Queen Victoria Market and finishing in the Docklands. It’s a relatively short walk, perfect for a pleasant afternoon exploring the city.

Begin your journey at the Queen Victoria Market. From here, head towards Flagstaff Gardens. The walk is approximately 0.562 km and should take around 7 minutes and 17 seconds. You’ll be enjoying a good start to your exploration!

Next, continue your walk to Flagstaff Gardens, a lovely park. This leg of the journey is a little longer, covering 1.182 km and taking about 15 minutes and 4 seconds. You’ll find yourself in a peaceful green space.

From Flagstaff Gardens, head towards Southern Cross Station. The walk is 1.55 km and should take around 18 minutes and 54 seconds. You’ll be passing through a busy transport hub.

Finally, you’ll arrive at the Docklands, a vibrant waterfront area. This final section is 1.55 km and should take around 18 minutes and 54 seconds.

The total distance of this walking route is 3.294 km and it will take approximately 41 minutes and 15 seconds to complete.

There are drinking water points located approximately 26 meters and 18 meters off the route. Restrooms are not directly located on this route.
