# Federation & Degraves: A Melbourne Walking Route

This route takes you through some of Melbourne’s most iconic landmarks, focusing on the historical significance of Federation and the vibrant atmosphere of Degraves Street. It’s a fantastic way to experience the heart of the city.

Begin your journey at the impressive Flinders Street Station, a designated landmark. From here, head towards Federation Square, a short walk of approximately 0.41 km, taking around 4 minutes and 52 seconds. You’ll arrive at a bustling public square – Federation Square.

Next, continue your exploration to St Paul’s Cathedral, a short distance of 0.288 km, which should take you around 4 minutes and 33 seconds. This beautiful cathedral offers a peaceful contrast to the surrounding city.

Finally, the last leg of your walk takes you to Degraves Street, a 3.215 km journey that will take approximately 42 minutes and 16 seconds. This vibrant street is known for its cafes and bustling atmosphere.

There’s a drinking water source approximately 14 meters off the route if you need a refreshment. The total walking distance for this route is 3.913 km, and it’s estimated to take around 51 minutes and 41 seconds.
