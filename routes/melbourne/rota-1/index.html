MELBOURNE · WALKING ROUTE

Melbourne walking route: Federation and Degraves

Distance 3.913 kmWalk 52 minStops 4Toilet / water 3

Hey! Fancy a little stroll around the City Centre? This is a really easy one, perfect for getting a feel for the place. We'll kick things off at the iconic Flinders Street Station – you can’t miss it! From there, it's a quick wander, just about 5 minutes and 0.4 km, to Federation Square. There's a public restroom and a drinking water point along the way, so handy if you need it.

Next up, it's another short hop, around 5 minutes and 0.3 km, to St Paul's Cathedral. Then, we’re off on a slightly longer leg – about 40 minutes and 3.2 km – to Degraves Street. It’s a lovely walk, so take your time and enjoy it! There are a few places to grab a drink along the way.

That's it! The whole loop is just under 4 km and should take about 50 minutes. Remember to wear comfy shoes, carry some water, and don't be afraid to stop and rest. There are public restrooms or drinking water points within 300 m of the route if you need them. Have a great time exploring!
