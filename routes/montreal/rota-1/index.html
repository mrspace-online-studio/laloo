# Exploring Montreal’s Historic Heart: A Walking Route

This route takes you through some of Montreal’s most iconic historic areas, starting with the magnificent Notre-Dame Basilica. Begin your journey here and head towards Place Jacques-Cartier. The walk is approximately 0.576 km and should take around 6 minutes and 45 seconds. You’ll be immersed in the atmosphere of this bustling area as you make your way to the next stop.

From Place Jacques-Cartier, continue your exploration towards the Old Port of Montreal. This leg of the walk is roughly 0.534 km and will take you around 6 minutes and 18 seconds. As you progress, you’ll find yourself surrounded by the vibrant energy of the port.

Next, you’ll journey to Chinatown Montreal. This part of the walk is the longest, covering 1.325 km, and will take approximately 16 minutes and 25 seconds. You’ll be experiencing a different cultural atmosphere as you move through the district.

Your complete walking tour will cover a total distance of 2.435 km and take approximately 29 minutes and 28 seconds.
