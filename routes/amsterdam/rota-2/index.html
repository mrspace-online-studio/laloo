Amsterdam walking route: Museumplein and Vondelpark

Distance 3.159 kmWalk 38 minStops 4Toilet / water 6

This walking route takes you through two of Amsterdam’s most iconic areas, starting with the impressive Rijksmuseum and finishing with the beautiful Vondelpark. The journey is approximately 3.159 km and will take you around 37 minutes to complete.

Begin your adventure at the Rijksmuseum. From here, you’ll head towards the Van Gogh Museum. The walk is a short 0.336 km, taking roughly 3 minutes and 58 seconds. This is a great opportunity to immerse yourself in Dutch art and history.

Next, you’ll move on to Vondelpark, a sprawling green space perfect for a relaxing stroll. This leg of the journey is a little longer, covering 1.438 km and taking approximately 16 minutes and 50 seconds. Enjoy the open space and perhaps a moment to rest.
