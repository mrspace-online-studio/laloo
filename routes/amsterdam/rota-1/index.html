amsterdam walking route: Merkez ve Jordaan · Laloo

2.335 km 28 min walk 4 stops 0 toilet / water

This route takes you through the heart of the Merkez and Jordaan districts of Amsterdam, starting from the bustling Amsterdam Centraal station. Begin your journey by heading west towards Dam Square. The walk from Amsterdam Centraal to Dam Square is approximately 0.93 kilometers and should take around 11 minutes and 16 seconds. You’ll find yourself in the iconic central square of Amsterdam, a great starting point for your exploration.

From Dam Square, continue your walk towards the north. The route leads you approximately 0.76 kilometers, taking around 9 minutes and 23 seconds. You'll arrive at the Westerkerk, a prominent church in the area. This is a key stop on your walking tour.

Next, you’ll walk another 0.642 kilometers, which takes approximately 7 minutes and 41 seconds. This will bring you to the Noorderkerk. This church is located in the Jordaan district, offering a change of scenery as you continue your exploration.

The total distance of this walking route is 2.335 kilometers, and it’s estimated to take around 28 minutes and 20 seconds to complete. Please note that there are no restrooms or water points recorded along this route.
