A Milanese Journey: Duomo & Sforza

This walking route will take you through some of Milan’s most iconic landmarks, starting with the magnificent Duomo. Begin your adventure at the Duomo di Milano, and head towards Piazza della Scala. The walk is approximately 7 minutes and 29 seconds, covering a distance of 0.62 kilometers.

Once you arrive at Piazza della Scala, continue your exploration towards Via Dante. This leg of the journey takes around 8 minutes and 45 seconds, and you’ll cover a distance of 0.721 kilometers. You’ll find yourself in the heart of Milan’s cultural district.

From Via Dante, the final section of your walk leads you to the impressive Castello Sforzesco. This walk is roughly 9 minutes and 15 seconds, and it’s approximately 0.701 kilometers long.

Your complete walking tour will take you a total of 25 minutes and 28 seconds, covering a distance of 2.042 kilometers. Please note that no restrooms or water points are recorded along this route.
