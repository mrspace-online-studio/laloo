Milan walking route: Kale ve park

3.179 km 39 min walk 4 stops 1 toilet / water

This walking route takes you through some of Milan’s most significant historical landmarks, culminating in a relaxing stroll through a beautiful park. It’s a fantastic way to get a feel for the city’s rich heritage and enjoy a little fresh air.

Your journey begins at the Castello Sforzesco, a formidable castle that serves as a starting point for your exploration. From here, you’ll head towards Parco Sempione, a short 0.43 kilometre walk, taking approximately 5 minutes and 18 seconds. This initial leg allows you to immediately immerse yourself in the heart of the city.

Next, you’ll move on to the Arco della Pace, a grand monument. The walk from Parco Sempione to the Arco della Pace is roughly 0.62 kilometres and will take around 7 minutes and 36 seconds. This section provides a gentle transition as you continue your exploration.

Finally, you’ll continue your walk to the Basilica di Sant’Ambrogio. This final leg covers 2.132 kilometres and will take around 25 minutes and 39 seconds. This segment offers a captivating end to your walk, bringing you to one of Milan’s oldest churches.

The total distance of this walking route is 3.18 kilometres, and the estimated walking time is 38 minutes and 33 seconds. A convenient spot to find refreshments is La Rocchetta, a cafe located approximately 20 metres off the route.
