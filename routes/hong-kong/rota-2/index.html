hong-kong walking route: Tsim Sha Tsui sahili · Laloo

2.279 km 31 min walk 5 stops 0 toilet / water

This walking route takes you through a fantastic section of Tsim Sha Tsui, offering a great way to experience some of Hong Kong’s key landmarks. The journey begins with a visit to the Clock Tower, a significant monument. From there, you’ll cover a short distance, approximately 1 minute and 15 seconds, to reach the Hong Kong Cultural Centre.

Next, you’ll continue your walk for around 10 minutes and 59 seconds, covering 0.683 km, to arrive at the Avenue of Stars. This park provides a lovely space to enjoy the waterfront views. From the Avenue of Stars, it’s a 14 minute and 19 second walk, spanning 1.155 km, to the Star Ferry Pier.

Your final stop is the 1881 Heritage, another important monument. The last leg of your journey is a quick 4 minute and 13 second walk, covering 0.337 km, to reach this location.

The entire route totals approximately 2.279 km and will take you around 30 minutes and 46 seconds to complete. Please note that no restrooms or water points are recorded along this route.
