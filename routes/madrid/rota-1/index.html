madrid walking route: Sanat üçgeni · Laloo

2.68 km 34 min walk 4 stops 1 toilet / water

This route takes you through three of Madrid’s most important art museums, culminating in a visit to the historic city squares. It’s a fantastic way to experience the city’s artistic heart, and the entire journey will take approximately 34 minutes.

Begin your exploration at the Museo del Prado. From here, you’ll want to head towards the Museo Thyssen-Bornemisza. The walk is a relatively short 0.685 kilometers, and should take around 8 minutes and 30 seconds. This initial leg sets the stage for a day of artistic discovery.

Next, continue your journey to Puerta del Sol. This stretch is a bit longer, covering 1.537 kilometers and taking around 19 minutes and 46 seconds. As you walk, you'll be immersed in the vibrant energy of the city.

Finally, you’ll arrive at Plaza Mayor. This final section is just 0.458 kilometers and should take approximately 5 minutes and 36 seconds. You’ve now completed the ‘Sanat Üçgeni’ route!

For a quick refreshment stop, La Venganza de Malinche café is located approximately 73 meters off the route.
