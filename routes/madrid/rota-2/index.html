# A Royal Stroll: Madrid’s Saray and Gran Via

This walking route takes you through some of Madrid’s most iconic landmarks, beginning with the grandeur of the Royal Palace. Start your journey at the Royal Palace of Madrid and head towards Plaza de Oriente. This leg will take you approximately 13 minutes and 36 seconds, covering a distance of 0.945 kilometers.

From Plaza de Oriente, continue your walk to the Teatro Real. This short hop will take you around 3 minutes and 59 seconds, covering a distance of just 0.275 kilometers. You’ll find yourself near this impressive building.

Next, it’s time to explore Plaza de España. This final section of your walk will take you around 12 minutes and 44 seconds, covering a distance of 0.926 kilometers. You’ll be able to enjoy the sights and sounds of this central plaza.

The entire route is approximately 2.146 kilometers and should take around 30 minutes and 19 seconds to complete. Please note that there are no restrooms or water points recorded along this walking route.
