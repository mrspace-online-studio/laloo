Bu metin, Vatikan çevresinde yaya profiline göre hesaplanmış taslak bir yürüyüş sırasını anlatır. Etiket: **Onaylı sıra (Uzay 13 Kas 2026)**. Vatikan Müzeleri durağı için giriş noktası OSM entrance etiketiyle belirlenmiştir; Sistine Şapeli, Rafael Odaları ve Pinacoteca ayrı durak değil, bu durağın içinde yer alır.

Üçüncü ara için yol üstü yardımcı tuvalet olarak Perfegg (kafe) listelenmiştir; rotadan uzaklığı 146,5 metredir. Kaynak notuna göre müşterilere açık olabilir, kesin bilgi yok. ## English (draft)

This text describes a draft walking sequence in the Vatican area, calculated for a pedestrian profile. Label: **Onaylı sıra (Uzay 13 Kas 2026)** (approved order). The route includes five main stops: Vatican Museums (entrance) (museum), St. Peter's Square (square), St. Peter's Basilica (basilica), Castel Sant'Angelo (castle), and Ponte Sant'Angelo (bridge). The Vatican Museums stop uses the entrance point identified by an OSM entrance tag; the Sistine Chapel, Raphael Rooms, and Pinacoteca are not separate stops but are inside this museum stop.

The consecutive walking segments are, in order: 0.963 km and 11 minutes 37 seconds; 0.402 km and 6 minutes 30 seconds; 1.324 km and 17 minutes 12 seconds; and 0.402 km and 4 minutes 41 seconds. The total distance is 3.091 kilometers and the total time is 40 minutes 0 seconds.

For the third segment, Perfegg (cafe) is listed as an on-route helper toilet, 146.5 meters from the route. The source note says it may be open to customers, but this is not confirmed. For the main stops, hours, fee, and accessibility are recorded as "bilgi yok" (no information). Although the stop order is approved by Uzay, the status remains draft; it must not be published without field verification and final publication approval.
