TOKYO · WALKING ROUTE

Tokyo walking route: Imperial Palace and gardens

Distance 2.679 kmWalk 39 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

This route takes you through some of Tokyo’s most significant historical and cultural landmarks, beginning at the bustling Tokyo Station and culminating at the iconic Nippon Budokan. It’s a fantastic way to experience a diverse range of spaces within the city.

Your journey begins at Tokyo Station, a major transportation hub. From here, you’ll walk approximately 1.253 kilometers, taking around 17 minutes and 50 seconds, to reach the Imperial Palace East Gardens. This initial leg offers a good introduction to the city’s scale and connects you to one of Japan’s most important historical sites.

Next, you’ll continue your walk for approximately 1.061 kilometers, a journey that will take you roughly 13 minutes and 48 seconds, to arrive at Kitanomaru Park. This park provides a pleasant transition and offers a different perspective on the city’s green spaces.

Finally, you’ll complete the route with a short 0.365 kilometer walk, lasting about 6 minutes and 55 seconds, to reach the Nippon Budokan. This impressive building is a key landmark and a testament to Japan's rich cultural heritage.

The entire route covers 2.679 kilometers and is estimated to take around 38 minutes and 32 seconds. Please note that restrooms and water sources are not available along this walking path.
