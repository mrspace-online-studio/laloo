Tokyo walking route: Harajuku and Yoyogi

Distance 3.158 kmWalk 42 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! So you’re doing the Harajuku and Yoyogi route – awesome choice! It’s a really nice, easy stroll, and you’ll get to see a bit of everything. Just wear comfy shoes, seriously, your feet will thank you. And definitely grab some water before you start, there's a water point along the way between Meiji Shrine and Yoyogi Park.

From Meiji Shrine, you’ll walk about 1.7 km to Yoyogi Park – it should take you around 20 minutes. It’s a pretty straightforward walk, just keep going until you reach the park. Then, it’s a shorter walk, about 1.1 km, to Harajuku Station, which will take about 15 minutes.

After Harajuku Station, it’s just a quick hop – only 0.4 km – to Takeshita Street, and that's only about 5 minutes. You’ll be done with the whole loop in roughly 40 minutes, give or take. There's a public restroom somewhere nearby, too, if you need it. Enjoy!
