Tokyo walking route: Asakusa and Ueno

Distance 3.126 kmWalk 40 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

This route takes you on a journey through the vibrant districts of Asakusa and Ueno, starting with the historic Senso-ji Temple and ending at the Tokyo National Museum. The entire walk is approximately 3.126 kilometers and should take around 39 minutes to complete.

Begin your adventure at Senso-ji, Tokyo’s oldest temple. From here, it’s a short and easy walk of 0.166 kilometers, taking around 2 minutes and 28 seconds, to Nakamise-dori, a bustling shopping street. This is a fantastic place to start your exploration.

Next, you’ll head towards Ueno Park. This leg of the journey covers 2.429 kilometers and will take you around 30 minutes. As you walk, you’ll pass through the heart of the area.

Finally, you’ll arrive at Ueno Park, where the Tokyo National Museum awaits. The last section is just 0.531 kilometers and will take approximately 7 minutes and 9 seconds.

Keep in mind that there’s a drinking water source approximately 17 meters off the route.
