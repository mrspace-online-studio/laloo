SEOUL · WALKING ROUTE

Seoul walking route: Gyeongbokgung and Insadong

Distance 3.294 kmWalk 40 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! So you're checking out this walk, great choice! It’s a lovely way to see a little bit of Seoul. We’ll start at Gyeongbokgung Palace, a beautiful spot to kick things off. From there, it's about a 20-minute walk, around 1.5 km, to Bukchon Hanok Village. Just wander along and enjoy the scenery – it’s a pretty easy stroll.

After you've enjoyed Bukchon Hanok Village, it’s another walk, this time about 15 minutes and 1.3 km, to Jogyesa Temple. You'll find a restroom along this leg, which is handy! Remember to wear comfy shoes, you’ll be doing a bit of walking.

The final stretch is a quick 5-minute walk, just half a kilometer, to Insadong. It’s a short hop from the temple, and you’ll be there before you know it. Carry some water with you, as there aren't any water fountains or restrooms close to the route. Enjoy your walk!
