Seoul walking route: City Hall and Myeongdong

Distance 3.512 kmWalk 50 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! So you want to do this little walk from City Hall to Myeongdong? It's a really nice way to see a bit of Seoul. You'll start at Seoul City Hall (bina), which is easy enough to find. Then you just wander on to Deoksugung Palace (palace). It's not a super long walk, just about 10 minutes, so you’ll be there before you know it. There’s a restroom along this first leg, which is handy.

From the palace, you'll head towards Myeongdong Cathedral (cathedral). This part is a little longer, around 20 minutes, and about 1.4 km. It’s a good idea to carry some water for this stretch, as there aren’t any water points nearby. You’ll walk on to the next stop after that.

Finally, you’ll finish up at Namsangol Hanok Village (bolge). The walk from the cathedral to the village is similar in distance, another 20 minutes and 1.4 km. The whole route is roughly 3.5 km and should take about 50 minutes total. Just wear comfy shoes, and take a break if you need one! Have fun!
