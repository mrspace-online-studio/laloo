SINGAPORE · WALKING ROUTE

Singapore walking route: Marina and river

Distance 2.528 kmWalk 34 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! Fancy a nice, easy walk along the Marina? It's a really pretty route, about 2.5 km total, and should take you around 35 minutes. We'll start at Merlion Park – you can’t miss it! Then we’ll just wander on to the Esplanade. That first leg is pretty short, only about 0.6 km and takes about 5 minutes.

Next up is the Helix Bridge. It's a bit further, around 0.9 km, so that’ll take about 10 minutes to reach. Remember to keep hydrated, as there aren't any water fountains or restrooms along any of the legs.

The final stretch takes us from the Helix Bridge to Gardens by the Bay, which is about 1.1 km and will take about 15 minutes. It's a lovely park, so feel free to take a break once you arrive.

Just a quick tip – wear comfy shoes, as you'll be doing a bit of walking! Have a great time!
