ROME · WALKING ROUTE

Rome walking route: Ancient Rome (long)

Distance 6.312 kmWalk 82 minStops 6Toilet / water 0

Hey! So you're doing the Ancient Rome route – awesome choice! It’s a bit of a longer one, but totally worth it. You’ll start at the Colosseum, a really impressive monument. From there, it's a short walk, only about 10 minutes, to the Roman Forum. It's just a quick hop from the Forum to the Palatine Hill, taking only about 5 minutes.

Then you’ll be heading towards the Circus Maximus. That leg is a bit longer, around 15 minutes, and there's a place to refill your water bottle along the way. After that, you'll walk to the Baths of Caracalla, another 15 minutes and another water point.

The final stretch to the Capitoline Hill is the longest, about 35 minutes, but it’s a really nice walk. There’s a water point along this leg too. Altogether, you're looking at around 80 minutes of walking and about 6.3 km.

Definitely wear comfy shoes for this one, and it’s a good idea to carry some water. Don’t forget to take breaks when you need them – there are public restroom or drinking water points within 300 m of the route. Enjoy the walk!
