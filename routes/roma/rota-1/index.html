ROME · WALKING ROUTE

Rome walking route: Ancient Rome

Distance 2.444 kmWalk 31 minStops 4Toilet / water 3

Hey! So you want to check out some of the ancient stuff, huh? This is a really nice little walk, perfect for getting a feel for the city. We’ll start at the Kolezyum, which is pretty impressive, and then you just wander on to the Forum Romanum. It’s only about a 10-minute stroll, around half a kilometre, and you'll find a place to grab some water and use the restroom along the way.

From the Forum Romanum, it’s a short hop – just 5 minutes – to Palatino Tepesi. This leg is only about 400 meters and there's a restroom if you need it. Then you’ll walk on to the Circus Maximus; it’s a bit further, about 1.4 kilometres and takes around 15 minutes.

The whole loop is about 2.4 kilometres and should take you roughly 30 minutes, but honestly, you'll probably want to take your time and soak it all in! Just wear comfy shoes – you’ll be doing a bit of walking – and maybe carry some extra water. There are restrooms and water points dotted around, but it’s always good to be prepared. Enjoy!
