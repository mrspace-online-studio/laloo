Antik Roma Yürüyüş Rotası

This walking route will take you on a journey through the heart of ancient Rome, exploring some of its most iconic landmarks. The route is approximately 2.444 kilometers and will take around 30 minutes to complete.

Begin your adventure at the Colosseum (anit). From here, you’ll walk approximately 0.646 kilometers, which should take around 8 minutes and 27 seconds. This initial stretch will lead you towards the Forum Romanum (antik_alan).

Once you arrive at the Forum Romanum (antik_alan), continue your exploration by walking 0.448 kilometers. This journey will take roughly 6 minutes and 19 seconds, bringing you to the Palatino Tepesi (antik_alan).

From the Palatino Tepesi (antik_alan), you’ll then walk 1.35 kilometers, a journey that will take about 16 minutes and 1 second. This final leg will bring you to the Circus Maximus (antik_alan).

Remember, there’s a drinking water source available approximately 45 meters off the route. Enjoy your exploration of ancient Rome!
