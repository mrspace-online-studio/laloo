ROME · WALKING ROUTE

Rome walking route: River and meydan

Distance 3.209 kmWalk 41 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

This route takes you on a journey through some of Rome’s most iconic locations, focusing on its beautiful waterways and grand squares. It’s a relatively short walk, perfect for experiencing the heart of the city.

Begin your exploration at Piazza del Popolo, a stunning square. From here, you’ll head towards the Ponte Sant'Angelo. The walk is approximately 1.682 kilometers and should take around 20 minutes and 45 seconds. This route will lead you directly to the historic bridge.

Next, you’ll continue on to Castel Sant’Angelo. This leg of the journey is just 0.402 kilometers and will take you around 5 minutes and 17 seconds. This impressive fortress is a key point of interest along the route.

Finally, you’ll make your way to Piazza Navona. This section is 1.125 kilometers and should take you approximately 14 minutes and 29 seconds. This vibrant square is a fantastic place to finish your walk.

Throughout your journey, you can find a cafe, Monte d'Oro, approximately 122 meters off the route. The total distance of the walk is 3.209 kilometers and will take around 40 minutes and 31 seconds.
