ROME · WALKING ROUTE

Rome walking route: Meydanlar and çeşmeler

Distance 2.069 kmWalk 27 minStops 4Toilet / water 3

This walking route takes you through some of Rome’s most iconic squares and monuments, offering a fantastic introduction to the city’s beauty. The journey begins at the vibrant Piazza Navona, a bustling central space. From here, you’ll head towards the Pantheon, a remarkable ancient structure.

Starting your walk, head approximately 0.582 kilometers towards the Pantheon, which should take around 7 minutes and 25 seconds. Once you arrive at the Pantheon, continue your exploration towards the Trevi Fountain. This leg of the journey is roughly 0.681 kilometers, and you can expect to spend around 8 minutes and 44 seconds walking.

Next, you’ll make your way to the Trevi Fountain, a famous fountain. From there, the final section of your walk will take you to the Spanish Steps, another key square. This final stretch is approximately 0.806 kilometers and will take around 10 minutes and 28 seconds.
