# Ermita ve Rizal: A Walking Journey

This route takes you through some of Manila’s most significant historical areas, beginning with Rizal Park. Start your exploration here and head towards the National Museum of Fine Arts. The walk is roughly 9 minutes and 27 seconds, covering a distance of 0.666 kilometers.

From the National Museum of Fine Arts, you’ll continue your journey towards Manila City Hall. This leg of the walk is approximately 7 minutes and 43 seconds, covering a distance of 0.481 kilometers.

Next, you’ll move on to Intramuros. This section of the route will take around 13 minutes and 27 seconds, and it’s a walk of 1.064 kilometers.

The total walking distance for this route is 2.211 kilometers, and it should take around 30 minutes and 38 seconds to complete.
