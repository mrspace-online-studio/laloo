Rizal Park & Manila’s Cultural Heart – A Walking Route

This route takes you on a journey through some of Manila’s most significant cultural landmarks, starting with the iconic Rizal Park. Begin your exploration here, and then head towards the National Museum of Fine Arts. The initial walk is a gentle introduction, covering approximately 0.666 kilometers and taking around 9 minutes and 27 seconds.

From the National Museum of Fine Arts, you’ll move on to the National Museum of Anthropology. This leg of the journey is shorter, just 0.297 kilometers, and should take you around 3 minutes and 53 seconds. It’s a quick transition to a completely different, fascinating collection.

Your final destination is Paco Park. This section of the walk is the longest, covering 1.307 kilometers and taking approximately 16 minutes and 44 seconds. It’s a good opportunity to stretch your legs and enjoy the change of scenery.

This walking route totals 2.27 kilometers and should take around 30 minutes and 5 seconds to complete. Please note that there are no restrooms or water points recorded along this route.
