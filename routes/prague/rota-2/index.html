# Exploring Prague: A Journey Through Castle and Mala Strana

This walking route takes you on a fascinating exploration of Prague’s historic heart, focusing on the iconic Prague Castle and the charming neighbourhood of Mala Strana. Get ready for a journey of approximately 32 minutes and covering a distance of 2.606 kilometers!

Your adventure begins at Prague Castle, a truly impressive landmark. From here, you’ll take a short walk of just 200 metres, taking around 2 minutes, to reach the magnificent St. Vitus Cathedral. This is a key destination within the castle complex, and a wonderful place to start your exploration.

Next, you’ll embark on a longer walk of around 23 minutes, covering 1.874 kilometres, to reach the famous Lennon Wall. This vibrant wall is a significant site, and a popular spot for artistic expression.

Finally, your route concludes with an 8 minute walk, covering 0.73 kilometres, to the beautiful Wallenstein Garden. This park offers a peaceful respite after your explorations.

Enjoy your walk!
