Prague walking route: Eski sehir ve meydanlar

2.46 km 30 min walk 4 stops 0 toilet / water

This walking route takes you through some of Prague’s most iconic landmarks, focusing on the city’s beautiful squares and historic buildings. It’s a fantastic way to get a feel for the city’s atmosphere and appreciate its rich heritage.

Begin your journey at Wenceslas Square, a bustling central location. From here, you’ll head towards the Municipal House, a significant historical building. The walk is approximately 13 minutes and covers a distance of 0.968 kilometers.

Next, you’ll move on to Old Town Square, another key area. This leg of the journey takes around 7 minutes and is just 0.581 kilometers long. This is a vibrant spot with plenty to see.

Finally, complete your tour by walking to Charles Bridge. This final section takes around 11 minutes, covering a distance of 0.911 kilometers.

The entire route is roughly 2.46 kilometers and should take you around 29 minutes to complete.

Restrooms and water points are not recorded along this route.
