VIENNA · WALKING ROUTE

Vienna walking route: Hofburg and Rathaus

Distance 2.017 kmWalk 26 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

This walking route will take you through some of Vienna’s most iconic landmarks, beginning with the impressive Hofburg Palace. Start your journey here and head towards Heldenplatz, a short 5 minutes and 20 seconds walk of approximately 0.465 kilometers. You’ll find yourself in a grand square, a perfect starting point for exploring the city’s history.

From Heldenplatz, continue your walk for around 12 minutes and 37 seconds, covering a distance of 0.959 kilometers. This will lead you to the magnificent Rathaus, a truly remarkable building. The route continues onwards, taking you another 7 minutes and 36 seconds to reach the Burgtheater.

Your final stop is the Burgtheater, another significant architectural landmark. This 0.593 kilometer walk will take you approximately 7 minutes and 36 seconds. You’ve now completed a rewarding walk of 2.017 kilometers, taking around 25 minutes and 33 seconds.

Please note that there are no restrooms or water points recorded along this route.
