VIENNA · WALKING ROUTE

Vienna walking route: Historic Center

Distance 2.207 kmWalk 27 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! Fancy a little stroll? This route takes you through some really lovely parts of the city centre. It’s a nice, easy one, perfect for getting a feel for the area. You’ll start at the impressive Stephansdom, and then it’s just a short walk to the Hofburg. It’s only about 0.8 km, so it'll take you around 10 minutes.

From the Hofburg, you’ll head towards the Vienna State Opera. It’s a quick hop – just 0.5 km and about 5 minutes. Remember to wear comfy shoes; you’ll be doing a little bit of walking! There aren't any water fountains or restrooms along this leg, so make sure you grab some water beforehand.

After the Opera, the final stretch takes you to Karlskirche. This leg is a little longer, around 0.9 km and about 10 minutes. The whole route is about 2.2 km and should take you roughly 25 minutes, but feel free to stop and soak it all in! Again, no restrooms or water points along the way. Enjoy!
