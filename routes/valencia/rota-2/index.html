VALENCIA · WALKING ROUTE

Valencia walking route: Turia parki and sanat

Distance 3.401 kmWalk 43 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! Fancy a nice, easy wander? This route’s a good one, showing off a bit of Valencia’s beauty. We’ll start at the Torres de Serranos, a really iconic landmark. Then we’ll head on to the Jardin del Turia, a huge park that used to be a riverbed – it’s lovely for a wander. It’s about 1.3 km, so should only take around 15 minutes.

From the park, we’ll walk to the Museo de Bellas Artes de Valencia. It's a bit further, around 1.6 km, and will take about 20 minutes. There's no water along this leg, so make sure you’ve got enough with you. You'll find a restroom somewhere along this walk too.

Our final stop is the Puente del Real, a beautiful bridge. It’s just a short hop from the museum, only 0.6 km and about 5 minutes. The whole route is around 3.4 km and takes about 45 minutes, so not too strenuous!

Definitely wear comfy shoes, and remember to take breaks as you go – enjoy the sunshine!
