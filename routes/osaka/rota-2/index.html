osaka walking route: Umeda ve Kita · Laloo

3.769 km 46 min walk 4 stops 0 toilet / water

This route takes you through the heart of Umeda and Kita Osaka, offering a chance to explore some key areas. It’s a pleasant walk of approximately 3.769 kilometers, taking around 46 minutes to complete.

Your journey begins at Osaka Station (meydan), a bustling transportation hub. From here, head west towards Grand Front Osaka. This short walk of 0.469 kilometers, taking about 5 minutes and 28 seconds, will lead you into a vibrant shopping and entertainment district.

Next, continue on to the Umeda Sky Building (anit). This walk from Grand Front Osaka is 0.561 kilometers and should take you around 6 minutes and 45 seconds. The Umeda Sky Building is a striking landmark, offering panoramic views of the city.

Finally, you’ll walk approximately 2.739 kilometers to Nakanoshima Park (park). This section of the route will take around 34 minutes and 5 seconds. Nakanoshima Park provides a tranquil space for a relaxing break.

Please note that no restrooms or water points were recorded along this route.
