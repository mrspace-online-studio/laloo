osaka walking route: Osaka Kalesi ve cevresi · Laloo

3.468 km 45 min walk 4 stops 0 toilet / water

This walking route takes you through the heart of Osaka, beginning with the iconic Osaka Castle and exploring the surrounding area. The journey begins with a short walk to Osaka Castle Park, offering a pleasant introduction to the city. From there, you’ll continue to the Osaka Museum of History, delving into the region’s past. Finally, the route concludes in the vibrant Osakajo-koen Station area.

Starting at Osaka Castle, you’ll immediately find yourself within the walls of the castle itself. A brief walk of just 0.319km will take you to the adjacent Osaka Castle Park. This initial leg takes approximately 4 minutes and 6 seconds to complete. Once in the park, you can enjoy the open space and take in the views of the castle.

Next, you’ll head towards the Osaka Museum of History, a journey of 1.098km which will take around 15 minutes and 13 seconds. The museum offers a fascinating look into Osaka’s past. Following your visit, you’ll walk approximately 2.051km to the Osakajo-koen Station area, an approximately 25 minute and 22 second walk.

The entire walking route covers a distance of 3.468km and is estimated to take around 44 minutes and 41 seconds. Please note that no restrooms or water sources are recorded along this route.
