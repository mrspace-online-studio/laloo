TAIPEI · WALKING ROUTE

Taipei walking route: 228 Park and Taipei Station

Distance 2.514 kmWalk 34 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! So you're doing the Laloo walk, awesome! It's a really nice little wander, perfect for getting a feel for the city. You start at 228 Peace Memorial Park, a lovely place to just breathe for a moment. Then you’ll walk for about 5 minutes, covering roughly 0.4 km, to the National Taiwan Museum.

From the museum, it's a bit of a longer stroll, about 10 minutes and 0.8 km, to Taipei Main Station. It’s a really central landmark, so you can’t miss it! There's a drinking water point along the way if you need a top-up.

The final leg takes you from Taipei Main Station to Zhongshan Hall, and that’s about 1.4 km and 15 minutes. The whole walk is only about 2.5 km and should take around 35 minutes, but don't rush it! There's a public restroom or drinking water point not too far from the route, just in case. Remember to wear comfy shoes, grab some water, and take breaks as needed. Enjoy!
