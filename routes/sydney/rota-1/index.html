SYDNEY · WALKING ROUTE

Sydney walking route: Harbor and Opera

Distance 2.155 kmWalk 28 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! Fancy a nice wander around the harbour? This little route is a really lovely way to see some of Sydney’s best bits. We’ll start at the iconic Sydney Opera House, you can't miss it! Then we’ll head on to the Royal Botanic Garden, it’s a nice easy walk of about 0.8 km and takes roughly 10 minutes. Remember to wear comfy shoes, you'll be glad you did!

From the Botanic Garden, we’ll stroll on to Circular Quay, which is about 1.0 km and takes around 15 minutes. There aren't any places to get water along this leg, so make sure you’ve got enough with you. A quick restroom break might be handy too, as there aren’t any nearby.

The final leg takes us from Circular Quay to The Rocks, just a short 0.4 km and about 5 minutes. The whole walk is just over 2.2 km and should take about 30 minutes – perfect for a relaxed afternoon. Have a great time!
