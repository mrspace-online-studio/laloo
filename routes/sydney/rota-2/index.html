Sydney walking route: Darling Harbour

Distance 2.711 kmWalk 39 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

This walking route offers a delightful journey through some of Sydney’s most iconic landmarks. It’s a manageable 2.711 kilometre adventure, taking approximately 38 minutes to complete. Let’s begin!

Your walk starts with a peaceful exploration of Hyde Park. From the park, head towards St Mary’s Cathedral. This short leg will take you just 0.38 kilometres and around 4 minutes and 39 seconds. You’ll find a beautiful building to admire as you make your way towards the next point.

Next, continue your journey to the Queen Victoria Building. This section stretches approximately 0.905 kilometres and should take you about 12 minutes and 3 seconds. You’ll pass by a significant historical structure during this part of the walk.

Finally, complete your route by heading towards Darling Harbour. This final leg is the longest, covering 1.426 kilometres and taking around 21 minutes and 58 seconds. You’ll arrive at a vibrant and bustling area.

There’s a water source available approximately 2 metres off the route, should you need a drink during your walk.
