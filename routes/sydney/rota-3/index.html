Sydney walking route: Botanic Garden and Domain

Distance 3.05 kmWalk 40 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

This route takes you on a pleasant walk through some of Sydney’s most beautiful parks and cultural landmarks. Starting at the Royal Botanic Garden Sydney, you’ll begin your exploration with a leisurely stroll, taking in the natural surroundings. The walk from the garden to Mrs Macquarie’s Chair is approximately 1.047 km and should take around 13 minutes and 25 seconds.

Next, you’ll head towards Mrs Macquarie's Chair, a historic sandstone bench offering stunning harbour views. From there, you’ll continue your journey to the Art Gallery of New South Wales, a short 1.292 km walk of around 16 minutes and 41 seconds. This will lead you into the heart of the city's cultural offerings.

Your final stop is the Hyde Park Barracks, a museum offering a glimpse into Sydney’s colonial past. This last leg of the route is just 0.711 km and should take you about 9 minutes and 37 seconds.

The total distance of this walking route is 3.05 km, and it’s estimated to take around 39 minutes and 43 seconds to complete.
