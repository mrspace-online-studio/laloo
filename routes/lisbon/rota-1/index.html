# A Lisbon Stroll: Alfama & Miradouros

This walk takes you through the heart of Alfama, Lisbon’s oldest district, offering stunning views and a genuine taste of the city. We’ll start at the iconic Lisbon Cathedral and end with the beautiful Miradouro da Graca. Let’s begin!

First, make your way from the Lisbon Cathedral to the Miradouro de Santa Luzia. This initial leg is approximately 0.478 kilometers and should take around 6 minutes and 54 seconds. As you walk, you'll be immersed in the traditional streets of Alfama, absorbing the atmosphere of this historic area.

Next, continue your journey from Miradouro de Santa Luzia to the Castelo de Sao Jorge. This section is a little longer, covering 0.712 kilometers, and will take you around 10 minutes and 42 seconds. The views become increasingly impressive as you approach the castle.

Finally, from Castelo de Sao Jorge, head towards the Miradouro da Graca. This final stretch is 1.061 kilometers and will take you around 14 minutes and 29 seconds. You’ll enjoy panoramic vistas of Lisbon as you arrive at this picturesque viewpoint.

The total walking distance for this route is 2.251 kilometers, and the estimated time is around 32 minutes and 5 seconds. Enjoy your exploration of Alfama and its beautiful miradouros!
