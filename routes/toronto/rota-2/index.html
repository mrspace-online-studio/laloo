TORONTO · WALKING ROUTE

Toronto walking route: Harbor and center

Distance 3.335 kmWalk 40 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! Fancy a nice stroll? This route, "Harbour and centre," which basically means "Harbour and City Centre," is a really lovely way to see a bit of Toronto. It's a fairly easy one, perfect for a sunny afternoon.

We’ll start at Union Station, a big landmark, and then it's a quick walk – about 10 minutes – to the CN Tower. From there, you’ll continue on to Harbourfront Centre, another 10 minutes or so. Remember to wear comfy shoes, you’ll be glad you did! There aren't any water fountains or restrooms along this first section, so carry some water with you.

Next up is St Lawrence Market, and that’s a bit further – around 20 minutes from Harbourfront Centre. It's a good idea to take a little break along the way, especially if it’s warm. Again, no restrooms or water points nearby, so keep that water handy.

The whole walk is about 40 minutes and just over 3km. Enjoy the views and the fresh air!
