TORONTO · WALKING ROUTE

Toronto walking route: Center and universiteler

Distance 2.731 kmWalk 34 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! Fancy a nice, easy walk around downtown? This route, which we call "City Centre and Universities," is a really pleasant way to see a few key spots. It’s not a long one, just about 2.7 km, so you should be able to do it in around 35 minutes. Definitely wear comfy shoes though – you’ll be on your feet for a bit!

We’ll start at Toronto City Hall, a pretty central landmark. From there, it's a quick 5-minute walk to Osgoode Hall. It’s only about 0.3 km, so you’ll be there in no time. After that, we’ll head to the Art Gallery of Ontario, which is a bit further, around 0.9 km and taking about 10 minutes. Remember to carry some water with you, as there aren't any water fountains or restrooms nearby along the way.

The final leg takes us to Queen's Park. It’s the longest stretch, at 1.5 km and about 20 minutes. Feel free to take a little break along the way; it's a lovely area to pause and enjoy. Have a great walk!
