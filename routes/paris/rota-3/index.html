Montmartre Walking Route

This route takes you through the heart of Montmartre, exploring some of its most iconic landmarks. It’s a fantastic way to experience the area’s unique atmosphere and enjoy a brisk walk. The total distance is approximately 2.047 kilometers and should take around 25 minutes to complete.

Begin your journey at the Sacré-Cœur Basilica. From here, head towards Place du Tertre. The walk is just 0.442 kilometers and will take you around 6 minutes. This bustling square is filled with artists creating and selling their work.

Next, continue your walk to the Moulin Rouge. This iconic building is approximately 0.951 kilometers away and will take you around 10 minutes and 48 seconds.

Finally, your last stop is the Wall of Love. This is a short 0.654 kilometer walk from the Moulin Rouge, which should take you about 8 minutes and 55 seconds.

Enjoy your exploration of Montmartre!
