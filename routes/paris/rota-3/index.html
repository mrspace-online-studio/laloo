Paris walking route: Montmartre

Distance 2.047 kmWalk 26 minStops 4Toilet / water 3

Hey! So you're wanting to explore Montmartre? This little walk is a great way to see a few highlights. It’s a pretty easy stroll, about 2 kilometers total and should take around 25 minutes, but honestly, you’ll want to linger! Definitely wear comfy shoes, you’ll be doing a bit of walking on uneven ground. And grab some water – there’s a drinking water point along the first leg, between the Sacré-Cœur Basilica and Place du Tertre.

From the Sacré-Cœur Basilica, you’ll just wander on to the Place du Tertre. It's only a short hop, about 5 minutes and less than half a kilometer. Then, it's a longer walk, around 1 kilometer, to the Moulin Rouge – that should take about 10 minutes. You'll find a public restroom along the way.

After the Moulin Rouge, it's another 10 minutes and about 700 meters to the Wall of Love. It's a pretty straightforward walk from there. There are a few other water points and restrooms close by, just in case you need them. Enjoy the views and have a great time!
