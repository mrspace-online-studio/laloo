A Journey Through Historic Paris

This walking route will take you through some of Paris’s most iconic historical sites, starting with the magnificent Notre-Dame de Paris. Begin your exploration by heading towards the impressive cathedral. The journey from Notre-Dame to Sainte-Chapelle is a relatively short one, covering 0.588 kilometers and taking approximately 9 minutes and 8 seconds.

Once you arrive at Sainte-Chapelle, you’ll be captivated by its stunning stained glass. From here, your walk continues to the Conciergerie, another significant historical monument. This leg of the journey is just 0.183 kilometers and should take around 2 minutes and 43 seconds.

The final part of your walk leads you to the renowned Louvre Museum. This section covers 1.315 kilometers and is expected to take around 16 minutes and 33 seconds. This will complete your journey through these important locations.

Throughout your walk, you’ll cover a total distance of 2.086 kilometers, and the entire route should take around 28 minutes and 24 seconds. A public restroom is located approximately 130 meters off the route.
