PARIS · WALKING ROUTE

Paris walking route: Louvre and Palais Royal

Distance 2.79 kmWalk 35 minStops 4Toilet / water 3

Hey! Fancy a nice wander through a pretty part of Paris? This route is a really easy one, perfect for a relaxed afternoon. We'll start at the Louvre Museum, then move on to the Palais Royal. It's a short hop, just about 5 minutes and half a kilometre – you'll find a public restroom and a water point along the way if you need them.

From the Palais Royal, we'll stroll to the Opéra Garnier. That’s a bit further, around 1.1 km and about 15 minutes of walking. It’s a really pleasant route! After that, it's onto the Jardin des Tuileries, another 1.3 km and 15 minutes. There’s a public restroom available on that leg too.

The whole loop is about 2.8 km, so should take around 35 minutes total. It's not a long walk, but you'll cover a good bit of ground! Just wear comfy shoes, carry some water, and don’t forget to take a break if you need one. There are a few restrooms and water points dotted nearby, too, if you need a refresh. Enjoy!
