Paris walking route: Louvre ve Palais Royal

2.79 km 35 min walk 4 stops 1 toilet / water

This walk takes you through some of Paris’s most iconic landmarks, beginning with the world-renowned Louvre Museum. Start your journey here and make your way towards the Palais Royal – it’s a relatively short distance of just 0.466km, and you should be able to cover it in approximately 5 minutes and 38 seconds.

From the Palais Royal, continue your exploration towards the magnificent Opéra Garnier. This leg of the walk is a little longer, stretching about 1.061km and taking around 12 minutes and 59 seconds. You’ll be rewarded with a stunning architectural experience.

Next, head towards the Jardin des Tuileries. This part of the route is the longest, covering 1.263km and taking around 16 minutes and 41 seconds. Enjoy the beautiful scenery as you make your way towards the park.

A quick refreshment stop is available at the nearby Tiger Sugar cafe, just 92 meters off the route. This walk totals 2.79km and will take you around 35 minutes and 18 seconds to complete.
