London walking route: Tower and Southwark

Distance 2.08 kmWalk 26 minStops 4Toilet / water 3

This walking route takes you through some of London’s most iconic landmarks, starting at the historic Tower of London and ending at the beautiful Southwark Cathedral. The journey is approximately 2.08 kilometres and should take around 25 minutes to complete. It’s a fantastic way to experience the city’s rich cultural heritage.

Begin your adventure at the Tower of London, a magnificent castle. From here, you’ll walk approximately 0.521 kilometres towards Tower Bridge. This short walk, lasting around 6 minutes and 54 seconds, will lead you directly to the impressive bridge.

Next, head to Borough Market, a bustling marketplace. From Tower Bridge, you’ll cover 1.371 kilometres, taking around 16 minutes and 25 seconds. The market is a vibrant hub of activity and a great place to explore.

Finally, your walk concludes with a brief 0.188 kilometre journey to Southwark Cathedral. This final leg, lasting just 2 minutes and 25 seconds, will bring you to the serene beauty of the cathedral.

A convenient spot to find refreshments is the Plants of Roselyn cafe, located approximately 16 metres off the route.
