LONDON · WALKING ROUTE

London walking route: Westminster and river

Distance 2.073 kmWalk 27 minStops 4Toilet / water 3

This walking route takes you through some of London’s most iconic landmarks, offering a fantastic experience along the river. It’s a relatively short journey, perfect for a pleasant afternoon.

Your adventure begins in St James’s Park. From here, you’ll walk approximately 1.3 kilometers, taking around 20 minutes to reach Westminster Abbey. This initial leg offers a beautiful introduction to the city, allowing you to enjoy the greenery and the surrounding atmosphere.

Next, you’ll move on to Westminster Abbey. The walk from Westminster Abbey to Westminster Bridge is just 0.5 kilometers, and you should be able to complete it in around 7 minutes. This short stretch brings you right to the heart of the action, placing you close to the famous bridge.
