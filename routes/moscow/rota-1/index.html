Moscow walking route: Kizil Meydan ve Kremlin

2.809 km 34 min walk 5 stops 0 toilet / water

This route will take you through some of Moscow’s most iconic landmarks, beginning with the bustling Okhotny Ryad. Start your adventure here and head towards the Alexander Garden – a pleasant walk of approximately 12 minutes and 38 seconds, covering 1.014 kilometers. You’ll find yourself in a beautiful park, a perfect place to begin your exploration.

Next, continue your walk to Red Square, which is roughly 14 minutes and 18 seconds away, spanning 1.188 kilometers. This historic square is the heart of Moscow and the starting point for many of the city’s most important sights. From here, you can easily reach Saint Basil’s Cathedral.

A short 2-minute, 18-second walk of just 0.19 kilometers will bring you to the vibrant colours of Saint Basil’s Cathedral. Finally, your journey concludes with a 5-minute, 2-second walk of 0.417 kilometers to the impressive GUM department store.

The entire walking route covers a total distance of 2.809 kilometers and will take approximately 34 minutes and 16 seconds to complete. Please note that no restrooms or water points were recorded along this route.
