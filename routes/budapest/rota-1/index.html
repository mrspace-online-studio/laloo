Budapest walking route: Parlamento ve bazilika

2.517 km 30 min walk 4 stops 0 toilet / water

This walking route offers a fantastic way to experience some of Budapest’s most iconic landmarks. Starting at the magnificent Hungarian Parliament building, your journey begins with a short, straightforward walk.

First, head towards Kossuth Lajos tér, a central square. The walk is just a few minutes, approximately 2 minutes and 10 seconds, covering a distance of 0.191 km. From here, you’ll continue to St. Stephen’s Basilica, a beautiful church. This leg of the journey takes around 14 minutes and 24 seconds and covers 1.187 km.

Next, you’ll make your way to the Széchenyi Chain Bridge, a historic structure. This final section of the walk is approximately 13 minutes and 43 seconds, covering a distance of 1.139 km.

The entire route totals 2.517 km and will take you around 30 minutes and 17 seconds to complete. Please note that there are no restrooms or water points recorded along this route.
