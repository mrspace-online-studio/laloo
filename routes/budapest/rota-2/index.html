BUDAPEST · WALKING ROUTE

Budapest walking route: Buda Castle and river

Distance 2.042 kmWalk 25 minStops 4Toilet / water 3

This route takes you on a journey through some of Budapest’s most iconic landmarks, starting with the historic Buda Castle and culminating at the magnificent Chain Bridge. It’s a fantastic way to experience the city’s rich cultural heritage.

Begin your exploration at Buda Castle, a significant site. From there, you’ll walk approximately 0.96 kilometers, taking around 12 minutes and 10 seconds. This initial leg leads you towards the impressive Matthias Church, another key destination in your walk.

Next, a short 0.064 kilometer stroll, lasting just 46 seconds, will bring you to the stunning Fisherman’s Bastion. This architectural marvel provides beautiful views of the city. Finally, you’ll continue for 1.018 kilometers, taking approximately 11 minutes and 53 seconds, to reach the impressive Chain Bridge.
