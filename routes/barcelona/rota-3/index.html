BARCELONA · WALKING ROUTE

Barcelona walking route: Waterfront and Ciutadella

Distance 3.976 kmWalk 49 minStops 4Toilet / water 2

This route offers a fantastic way to experience Barcelona, taking you from the grand Arc de Triomf to the lively shores of Barceloneta Beach. It’s a relatively easy walk, perfect for a pleasant afternoon exploring the city.

Your journey begins at the impressive Arc de Triomf. From here, head east along a straight path for approximately 803 metres. This walk will take you around 10 minutes and 12 seconds, leading you directly into the beautiful Parc de la Ciutadella.

Next, you’ll continue your walk, heading southeast for 1.896 kilometres. This section will take you around 23 minutes and 10 seconds. You’ll arrive at Port Olímpic, a vibrant waterfront area.
