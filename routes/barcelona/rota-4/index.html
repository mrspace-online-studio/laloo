# Gaudi’s Barcelona: A Walking Route

This route will take you past three of Antoni Gaudi’s most iconic buildings, offering a fantastic introduction to his unique architectural style. The walk itself is a relatively short one, taking around 27 minutes to complete.

Begin your journey at Casa Batlló, a remarkable monument. From there, head approximately 0.543 km, which should take you around 7 minutes and 6 seconds. You’ll be making your way towards Casa Milà.

Once you reach Casa Milà, continue your walk for approximately 1.696 km. This leg of the journey will take around 20 minutes and 36 seconds.

Finally, you’ll arrive at the Sagrada Família, a stunning basilica. This completes your walking route, covering a total distance of 2.239 km and taking approximately 27 minutes and 42 seconds.

Please note that no restrooms or water points are recorded along this route.
