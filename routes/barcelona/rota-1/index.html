BARCELONA · WALKING ROUTE

Barcelona walking route: Gotik district

Distance 2.304 kmWalk 29 minStops 4Toilet / water 6

This walk takes you through a vibrant section of Barcelona, exploring architectural highlights and bustling markets. The route is approximately 2.304 km and will take you around 28 minutes to complete.

Begin your journey at the impressive Barcelona Cathedral (bazilika). From here, head towards the Palau de la Música Catalana (monument), a short 0.641 km walk, which should take you around 7 minutes and 43 seconds. You’ll be rewarded with a stunning architectural masterpiece.

Next, continue your exploration to Placa Reial (meydan), a little further on at 1.135 km, approximately 13 minutes and 56 seconds. This beautiful square is a fantastic place to take a breather and soak in the atmosphere.
