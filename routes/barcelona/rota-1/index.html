barcelona walking route: Gotik mahalle · Laloo

2.304 km 29 min walk 4 stops 0 toilet / water

This walk takes you through a vibrant section of Barcelona, exploring architectural highlights and bustling markets. The route is approximately 2.304 km and will take you around 28 minutes to complete.

Begin your journey at the impressive Barcelona Cathedral (bazilika). From here, head towards the Palau de la Música Catalana (anit), a short 0.641 km walk, which should take you around 7 minutes and 43 seconds. You’ll be rewarded with a stunning architectural masterpiece.

Next, continue your exploration to Plaça Reial (meydan), a little further on at 1.135 km, approximately 13 minutes and 56 seconds. This beautiful square is a fantastic place to take a breather and soak in the atmosphere.

Finally, complete the loop by walking to Mercat de la Boqueria (meydan), just 0.528 km away, and this should take you around 7 minutes and 7 seconds. Enjoy the sights, sounds, and smells of this lively market.
