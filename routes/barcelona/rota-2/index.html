Barcelona walking route: Passeig de Gracia

2.723 km 34 min walk 4 stops 1 toilet / water

This route offers a delightful exploration of Barcelona's architectural gems, inspired by the vibrant spirit of Turkish design. Let’s begin our journey!

First, we’ll start at Casa Batlló, a truly remarkable building. From here, we’ll head towards Casa Milà, a short 0.543 km walk, taking approximately 7 minutes and 6 seconds. This leg of the journey will take you through the heart of the city.

Next, we’ll continue our exploration to Plaça de Catalunya, a bustling square. This section is a slightly longer walk of 1.354 km, and should take around 16 minutes and 6 seconds. You’ll find yourself surrounded by the energy of the city.

Finally, our route concludes with a 0.826 km walk to Casa Amatller, another beautiful building. This final leg will take around 11 minutes and 5 seconds.

The total distance of this walking route is 2.723 km, and it will take approximately 34 minutes and 17 seconds to complete. A Starbucks cafe is located approximately 91 meters from the route, offering a convenient place to refresh.
