ZURICH · WALKING ROUTE

Zurich walking route: Old Town and göl

Distance 2.11 kmWalk 26 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

This walking route takes you through the heart of Zurich, beginning with a visit to the iconic Hauptbahnhof. Start at Zürich HB and head towards Bahnhofstrasse, a distance of approximately 0.764 kilometers. This walk will take you around 9 minutes and 49 seconds. You’ll find yourself on Bahnhofstrasse, a bustling main street.

From Bahnhofstrasse, continue your journey to Bürkliplatz, another vibrant square. This section of the route is roughly 0.748 kilometers and should take you around 9 minutes and 5 seconds. Bürkliplatz is a key meeting point and a great place to experience the city’s atmosphere.

Your final destination is the Grossmünster, a magnificent cathedral. The walk from Bürkliplatz to the Grossmünster is approximately 0.598 kilometers, and you can expect to spend about 7 minutes and 36 seconds covering this distance.

The entire route covers a distance of 2.11 kilometers and should take approximately 26 minutes and 30 seconds to complete. Please note that no restrooms or water points were recorded along this walking route.
