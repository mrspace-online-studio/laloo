ZURICH · WALKING ROUTE

Zurich walking route: Limmat and Rathaus

Distance 2.676 kmWalk 33 minStops 4Toilet / water 0

Hey! Fancy a nice wander? This is a really pleasant route I often do. It starts at the Zurich Main Station, a pretty busy spot, and then you just stroll along to Bahnhofstrasse. It’s a short walk, only about 10 minutes and just under a kilometer.

From the square of Bahnhofstrasse, it’s a quick 5 minutes to Lindenhof, a lovely park. It's a really easy walk, you'll be there before you know it! Then you continue on to Rathaus Zürich, which is about 15 minutes away and a bit further, around 1.5 kilometers. There's a drinking water point along that stretch, so it's good to keep an eye out.

The whole walk is just under 3 kilometers and should take around 35 minutes, but don't rush! There’s a public restroom near the route too, if you need it. Make sure you wear comfy shoes, carry some water, and take breaks if you need them. Enjoy!
