lyon walking route: Presqu ile merkez · Laloo

2.088 km 26 min walk 4 stops 0 toilet / water

This walking route takes you through some of Lyon’s most iconic squares, offering a fantastic introduction to the city’s central area. The route is approximately 2.088 km and should take around 25 minutes to complete.

Begin your journey at Place Bellecour. From here, head east towards Place des Jacobins. The walk is just over half a kilometre and should take around 5 minutes and 27 seconds. You’ll find yourself immersed in the vibrant atmosphere of this central square.

Next, continue your walk to the Hôtel de Ville de Lyon. This leg of the journey is a little longer, covering 0.449 km and taking approximately 5 minutes and 25 seconds. The impressive city hall stands as a focal point of Lyon’s civic life.

Finally, complete the loop by heading towards Place des Terreaux. This section is the longest, covering 1.211 km and taking around 14 minutes and 46 seconds. You’ll arrive at another significant square, known for its architecture.

Enjoy your exploration of Lyon’s historic heart!
