Walking in Paris: FAQ

What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes in Paris. These routes highlight locations of public restrooms and drinking water points along the way, helping you stay comfortable during your explorations.

How many walking routes are available in Paris on Laloo?

Currently, Laloo provides three walking routes for exploring Paris. These routes offer different perspectives on the city and its landmarks.

What are the themes of the Paris walking routes?

The routes are themed 'Historical Centre and River', 'Louvre and Palais Royal', and 'Montmartre'. These themes guide you through distinct areas of Paris, showcasing various points of interest.

How long is the 'Historical Centre and River' route?

The 'Historical Centre and River' route covers a distance of 2.086 kilometers, which takes approximately 28 minutes and 24 seconds to complete at a comfortable walking pace.

What landmarks are included on the 'Louvre and Palais Royal' route?

This route features the Louvre Museum, Palais Royal, Opéra Garnier, and the Jardin des Tuileries. It’s a great way to see these significant Parisian locations.

Does Laloo provide information on restroom and water facilities along the routes?

Yes, Laloo highlights the locations of public restrooms and drinking water points along the routes. However, for Route 3 ('Montmartre'), no such points have been recorded.

Does Laloo verify opening hours or accessibility of the stops on the routes?

Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility of the landmarks and locations included on the walking routes.
