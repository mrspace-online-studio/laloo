Walking in Zurich: FAQ

What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes. These routes highlight locations of restrooms and drinking water sources along the way, to help you plan your journey.

Where can I find Laloo walking routes?

Currently, Laloo provides walking routes in Zurich. There are two routes available for exploration in this city.

What are the themes of the Zurich walking routes?

Route 1 focuses on the old city and lake. Route 2 explores the Limmat River and the city hall area. Please note the original themes may have been in Turkish.

How long is Route 1?

Route 1 is 2.11 kilometers long and takes approximately 26 minutes and 30 seconds to complete at a normal walking pace.

How long is Route 2?

Route 2 is 2.676 kilometers long, and it typically takes around 33 minutes and 2 seconds to walk the entire route.

Are there any restroom or drinking water facilities along the routes?

Route 1 does not have any recorded restroom or drinking water points. Route 2 has drinking water points available along the route.

Does Laloo verify the accessibility of the stops on the routes?

Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility of the stops included in the walking routes.
