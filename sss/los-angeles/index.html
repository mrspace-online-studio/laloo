# Walking in Los Angeles: FAQ

## What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes in Los Angeles. These routes highlight locations of restrooms and drinking water sources nearby, helping you plan your walk comfortably.

## How many walking routes are available in Los Angeles on Laloo?

Currently, Laloo provides three walking routes in Los Angeles. You can explore downtown, Hollywood Boulevard, and central Santa Monica with these guided paths.

## What are the stops along the 'Downtown merkez' route?

The 'Downtown merkez' route includes stops at Union Station, Olvera Street, Grand Park, and the Walt Disney Concert Hall. The route covers a distance of 2.086 km and takes approximately 26 minutes and 25 seconds to complete.

## Does the 'Hollywood bulvarı' route have any places to get refreshments?

Yes, the 'Hollywood bulvarı' route includes locations where you can find refreshments. Specifically, there are recorded restroom and water points at a Starbucks and The Pie Hole.

## How long is the 'Santa Monica merkezi' route?

The 'Santa Monica merkezi' route is the longest of the three, spanning 3.023 km. It’s estimated to take around 38 minutes and 11 seconds to walk the entire route.

## Are restroom and water points guaranteed to be available?

Laloo routes indicate locations of restrooms and drinking water, but Laloo does not verify the availability of these facilities. It’s always a good idea to confirm before you rely on them.

## Does Laloo provide information about opening hours or accessibility of the stops?

No, Laloo does not provide details about the opening hours, fees, or accessibility of the locations featured on its walking routes.
