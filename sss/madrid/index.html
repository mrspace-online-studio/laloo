# Walking in Madrid: FAQ

## What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes. These routes highlight locations of restrooms and drinking water sources along the way, making your explorations more comfortable.

## Where can I find Laloo routes?

Currently, Laloo provides walking routes in Madrid. There are two routes available for you to explore within the city.

## How long is the 'Sanat üçgeni' (Art Triangle) route?

The 'Sanat üçgeni' route, which focuses on art and landmarks, is 2.68 kilometers long. It typically takes around 33 minutes and 52 seconds to complete at a normal walking pace.

## What are the stops on the 'Sanat üçgeni' route?

The 'Sanat üçgeni' route includes stops at the Museo del Prado, Museo Thyssen-Bornemisza, Puerta del Sol, and Plaza Mayor. These locations offer a varied experience within Madrid.

## What about the 'Saray ve Gran Via' (Palace and Gran Via) route?

The 'Saray ve Gran Via' route covers 2.146 kilometers and usually takes approximately 30 minutes and 19 seconds to walk. It passes by the Royal Palace of Madrid, Plaza de Oriente, Teatro Real, and Plaza de España.

## Are there any restroom or water points listed on the 'Saray ve Gran Via' route?

Currently, no restroom or water points have been recorded along the 'Saray ve Gran Via' route. Please plan accordingly.

## Does Laloo provide information about opening hours or fees for the stops on its routes?

Laloo does not verify opening hours, fees, or accessibility details for the locations featured on its walking routes. It is recommended to check this information separately before you go.
