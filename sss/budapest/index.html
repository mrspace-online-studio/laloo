Walking in Budapest: FAQ

What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes. These routes highlight locations of restroom and drinking water facilities near the path you’ll take. You can find Laloo at https://www.laloo.org.

Where can I find Laloo routes?

Currently, Laloo provides walking routes specifically for Budapest.

How many walking routes are available in Budapest?

There are two walking routes presently available for Budapest users on Laloo.

What are the names of the Budapest walking routes?

The routes are called 'Parliament and Basilica' and 'Buda Castle and the River'.

What landmarks are included in the 'Parliament and Basilica' route?

This route visits the Hungarian Parliament, Kossuth Lajos tér, St. Stephen's Basilica, and Széchenyi Chain Bridge.

How long is the 'Buda Castle and the River' route?

The 'Buda Castle and the River' route is 2.042 kilometers long and takes approximately 24 minutes and 48 seconds to complete by walking.

Are restroom and water facilities available along the Laloo routes in Budapest?

Currently, no restroom or drinking water points have been recorded along either of the Budapest routes.
