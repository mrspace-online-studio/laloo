# Walking in Seoul: FAQ

## What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes in Seoul. It helps you explore the city on foot, highlighting locations of restroom and drinking water points along the way.

## How many walking routes are available in Seoul on Laloo?

Currently, there are two walking routes available in Seoul through Laloo.

## Can you tell me about the 'Gyeongbokgung ve Insadong' route?

This route, translating to 'Gyeongbokgung and Insadong', is 3.294 km long and takes approximately 40 minutes and 20 seconds to complete. It includes stops at Gyeongbokgung Palace, Bukchon Hanok Village, Jogyesa Temple, and Insadong.

## What is included in the 'City Hall ve Myeongdong' route?

The 'City Hall and Myeongdong' route spans 3.512 km and takes about 49 minutes and 35 seconds to walk. You'll pass by Seoul City Hall, Deoksugung Palace, Myeongdong Cathedral, and Namsangol Hanok Village.

## Are restroom and water facilities available along the routes?

Unfortunately, Laloo has not recorded any restroom or drinking water points along either of the Seoul walking routes.

## Does Laloo provide information about opening hours or fees for the stops on its routes?

Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility of the locations featured on its walking routes.
