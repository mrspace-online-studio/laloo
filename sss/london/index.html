Walking in London: FAQ

What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes in London. These routes highlight locations of restrooms and drinking water sources near the paths you’ll take. You can find more information at https://www.laloo.org.

How many walking routes are available in London on Laloo?

Currently, Laloo features three walking routes specifically designed for London. These routes offer different experiences and cover various areas of the city.

Could you tell me about the 'Westminster ve nehir' route?

The 'Westminster ve nehir' route takes you past notable landmarks including St James's Park, Westminster Abbey, Westminster Bridge, and the London Eye. The route covers a distance of 2.073 km and takes approximately 27 minutes and 15 seconds to complete.

Does the 'Covent Garden ve British Museum' route include restrooms or water points?

No, the 'Covent Garden ve British Museum' route does not currently have any recorded restroom or drinking water points listed nearby. The route itself is 2.533 km long and should take around 31 minutes and 24 seconds to walk.

What’s on the 'Tower ve Southwark' route?

The 'Tower ve Southwark' route guides you past the Tower of London, Tower Bridge, Borough Market, and Southwark Cathedral. This route is 2.08 km long and should take roughly 25 minutes and 44 seconds to complete.

Are there any facilities available along the 'Tower ve Southwark' route?

Yes, Plants of Roselyn, a cafe, is listed as a location providing restroom and drinking water facilities near the 'Tower ve Southwark' route.

Does Laloo provide information about opening hours for the places on the routes?

Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility of the stops featured on its walking routes. It’s recommended to check these details directly with the locations themselves.
