# Laloo FAQ

## What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website designed to help you find the nearest public restrooms, cafés, and local tips. It provides a map-based location of these places, offering a convenient way to locate a bathroom quickly.

## How do I use Laloo?

You can use Laloo directly on the website. It offers a map with locations of public restrooms, cafés, and water stops. You can also explore saved locations and discover new ones through the ‘Explore’ feature.

## What kind of routes are available on Laloo?

Laloo offers a selection of walking routes, currently featuring routes from cities like Barcelona, Berlin, London, Los Angeles, Madrid, Melbourne, Milan, Paris, Rome, Sydney, Taipei, Tokyo, Vatican City, Zurich, and more.

## Can I download an app for Laloo?

No, Laloo does not require you to download an app. You can access all its features directly through the website at https://www.laloo.org.

## How can I contribute to Laloo?

You can help Laloo by adding new bathroom locations or sharing stories about interesting places you’ve found. You can also provide tips for others and add a photo of the door or sign.

## What does the ‘Surprise me’ feature do?

The ‘Surprise me’ feature on Laloo will randomly suggest a nearby bathroom or tip, helping you discover new locations you might not have known about. It’s a great way to explore your surroundings.

## How can I find information about a specific place?

You can use the ‘Where is it?’ feature to locate a specific place on the map. You can also add your own tip for others, providing helpful information about the location.
