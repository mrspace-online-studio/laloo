# Walking in Toronto: FAQ

## What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes in Toronto. It helps you explore the city on foot, highlighting locations of restrooms and drinking water along the way.

## How many walking routes are available in Toronto on Laloo?

Currently, there are two walking routes available in Toronto through Laloo.

## What are the names of the Toronto walking routes?

The routes are named 'Merkez ve universiteler' and 'Liman ve merkez'.

## What landmarks are included in the 'Merkez ve universiteler' route?

This route includes Toronto City Hall, Osgoode Hall, the Art Gallery of Ontario, and Queen's Park.

## How long is the 'Liman ve merkez' route?

The 'Liman ve merkez' route covers a distance of 3.335 km and takes approximately 39 minutes and 45 seconds to complete.

## Are restroom and drinking water points listed for the Toronto routes?

No restroom or drinking water points have been recorded along either of the Toronto walking routes.

## Does Laloo provide information about the opening hours or fees for the landmarks on its routes?

Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility details for the locations featured on its walking routes.
