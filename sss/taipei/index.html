# Walking in Taipei: FAQ

## What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes. These routes are designed to help you explore cities, with a focus on identifying locations where you can find restrooms and drinking water along the way.

## Where are Laloo routes available?

Currently, Laloo provides walking routes in Taipei. There are two routes available for exploration within the city.

## How many walking routes are there in Taipei?

There are two walking routes currently available in Taipei through Laloo.

## What are the stops on the 'Ximending ve anıtlar' route?

The 'Ximending ve anıtlar' route includes stops at the Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall, 228 Peace Memorial Park, the Presidential Office Building, and Ximending.

## How long is the '228 Park ve Taipei Station' route?

The '228 Park ve Taipei Station' route covers a distance of 2.514 km and takes approximately 34 minutes and 14 seconds to complete at a normal walking pace.

## What facilities are indicated on the routes?

Laloo routes highlight locations providing drinking water and restrooms. The 'Ximending ve anıtlar' route indicates drinking water points, while the '228 Park ve Taipei Station' route shows a 7-Eleven.

## Does Laloo provide information about opening hours or fees for the stops on the routes?

Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility of the locations featured on its walking routes.
