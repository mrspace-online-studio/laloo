# Walking in Melbourne: FAQ

## What is Laloo?

Laloo provides walking routes in Melbourne. The website, found at https://www.laloo.org, highlights locations of restroom and drinking water points along these routes.

## How many walking routes are available in Melbourne through Laloo?

Currently, there are two walking routes available in Melbourne on Laloo.

## Can you tell me about the 'Federation ve Degraves' route?

This route, translating to 'Federation and Degraves', covers a distance of 3.913 km and takes approximately 51 minutes and 41 seconds to complete. It includes stops at Flinders Street Station, Federation Square, St Paul's Cathedral, and Degraves Street. Drinking water is available along the route.

## What are the stops on the 'Docklands ve Flagstaff' route?

The 'Docklands and Flagstaff' route includes stops at Queen Victoria Market, Flagstaff Gardens, Southern Cross Station, and Docklands. This route is 3.294 km long and takes roughly 41 minutes and 15 seconds to walk. You can find drinking water along the way.

## Does Laloo provide information about the opening hours of the stops on its routes?

Laloo does not verify the opening hours of the locations featured on its walking routes.

## Does Laloo confirm the accessibility of the stops?

No, Laloo does not verify the accessibility of the stops included on its walking routes.

## Are there any fees associated with using the Laloo walking routes?

Laloo does not provide information about any fees that may be associated with visiting the stops on its routes.
