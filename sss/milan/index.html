Walking in Milan: FAQ

What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes. These routes highlight locations of restrooms and drinking water sources along the way, making your explorations more comfortable.

Where can I find Laloo routes?

Currently, Laloo provides walking routes specifically in Milan. There are two routes available for you to explore within the city.

How long is the 'Duomo ve Sforza' route?

The 'Duomo ve Sforza' route covers a distance of 2.042 kilometers. It typically takes approximately 25 minutes and 28 seconds to complete the walk.

What places does the 'Kale ve park' route visit?

The 'Kale ve park' route includes stops at Castello Sforzesco, Parco Sempione, Arco della Pace, and Basilica di Sant'Ambrogio. It's a longer walk, taking around 38 minutes and 33 seconds.

Are there any restroom or drinking water locations on the routes?

On the 'Duomo ve Sforza' route, no restroom or drinking water points have been recorded. However, the 'Kale ve park' route includes La Rocchetta, which is listed as a location for these amenities.

Does Laloo provide information about opening hours or fees for the stops on the routes?

Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility details for the locations featured on the walking routes. It's recommended to check this information separately before your visit.
