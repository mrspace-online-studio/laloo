Walking in Brisbane: FAQ

What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes in Brisbane. It helps you explore the city on foot, highlighting locations of restroom and drinking water points along the way.

How many walking routes are available in Brisbane on Laloo?

Currently, there are two walking routes available on Laloo for Brisbane. You can choose from routes themed 'South Bank and the River' or 'Botanical Garden and City Centre'.

Can you tell me more about the 'South Bank and the River' route?

This route covers 2.494 kilometers and takes approximately 30 minutes and 54 seconds to complete. It passes by the Queensland Performing Arts Centre, South Bank Parklands, Queensland Museum, Victoria Bridge, and Brisbane City Hall.

What about the 'Botanical Garden and City Centre' route? What does it include?

This route is 3.182 kilometers long and takes about 41 minutes and 36 seconds to walk. It includes stops at the City Botanic Gardens, Parliament House, Queen Street Mall, ANZAC Square, and Roma Street Parkland.

Are there restrooms or water fountains along the routes?

Unfortunately, Laloo has not recorded any restroom or drinking water points along either of the Brisbane walking routes. You will need to check independently for facilities at the stops.

Does Laloo provide information about opening hours or fees for the places visited on the routes?

Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility of the locations featured on its walking routes. It is your responsibility to check this information before you go.
