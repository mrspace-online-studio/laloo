Walking in Vienna: FAQ

What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes. These routes highlight locations of restrooms and drinking water sources nearby. You can find walking routes for Vienna on the Laloo website.

How many walking routes are available in Vienna?

Currently, there are two walking routes available in Vienna on Laloo.

What are the stops on the 'Tarihî merkez' route?

The 'Tarihî merkez' route includes stops at Stephansdom, Hofburg, Vienna State Opera, and Karlskirche.

How long does it take to walk the 'Hofburg ve Rathaus' route?

The 'Hofburg ve Rathaus' route takes approximately 25 minutes and 33 seconds to complete on foot.

What is the length of the 'Tarihî merkez' route?

The 'Tarihî merkez' route covers a distance of 2.207 kilometers.

Are restroom and drinking water locations available along the routes?

Laloo routes in Vienna do not currently have any recorded restroom or drinking water points noted along the paths.
