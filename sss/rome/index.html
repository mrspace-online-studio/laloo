Walking in Rome: FAQ

What is Laloo and where can I find it?

Laloo provides walking routes, and you can find it online at https://www.laloo.org. These routes highlight locations where you can find restrooms and drinking water along the way.

How many walking routes are available in Rome?

Currently, there are four walking routes available in Rome through Laloo.

What are the themes of the walking routes in Rome?

The routes explore themes such as Ancient Rome, squares and fountains, the river and squares, and a longer exploration of Ancient Rome.

Could you tell me about the 'Antik Roma' route?

The 'Antik Roma' route, or Ancient Rome route, includes stops at the Colosseum, Roman Forum, Palatine Hill, and Circus Maximus. The route is 2.444 km long and takes approximately 30 minutes and 47 seconds to complete. Drinking water is available along the route.

Does every route have facilities available?

Not every route has recorded restroom or drinking water points. Some routes, like the one focusing on squares and fountains, do not have any facilities listed.

What can you tell me about the 'Nehir ve meydan' route?

The 'Nehir ve meydan' route, or River and Squares route, visits Piazza del Popolo, Ponte Sant'Angelo, Castel Sant'Angelo, and Piazza Navona. The route covers 3.209 km and takes roughly 40 minutes and 31 seconds. A cafe, Monte d'Oro, provides water along the route.

What is the longest route available in Rome?

The 'Ancient Rome (long)' route is the longest, covering 6.312 km and taking approximately one hour and 21 minutes and 36 seconds. It includes stops at several ancient sites and provides access to drinking water and public restrooms.
