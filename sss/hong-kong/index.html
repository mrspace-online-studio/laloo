# Walking in Hong Kong: FAQ

## What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes. It helps you explore Hong Kong, with a focus on locating restrooms and drinking water sources along the way. You can find Laloo at https://www.laloo.org.

## How many walking routes are available in Hong Kong?

Currently, there is one walking route available in Hong Kong through Laloo.

## Can you tell me about Route 1?

Route 1, themed 'Tsim Sha Tsui Coastline,' includes stops at the Clock Tower, Hong Kong Cultural Centre, Avenue of Stars, Star Ferry Pier, and 1881 Heritage. The route covers 2.279 kilometers and takes approximately 30 minutes and 46 seconds to complete.

## Are there restrooms or drinking water points on Route 1?

Unfortunately, Laloo has not recorded any restroom or drinking water points along Route 1.

## Does Laloo verify the opening hours or fees for the places featured on its routes?

No, Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility of the locations included in its walking routes.

## What is 1881 Heritage?

1881 Heritage is a monument featured as a stop on Laloo’s Route 1.

## What is the Avenue of Stars?

The Avenue of Stars is a park that is one of the stops on Laloo’s Route 1.
