# Walking in Barcelona: FAQ

## What is Laloo?

Laloo provides walking routes in Barcelona. These routes highlight locations of restroom and drinking water facilities nearby, making your explorations more comfortable.

## How many walking routes are available in Barcelona through Laloo?

Currently, there are four walking routes available in Barcelona on Laloo.

## Can you tell me about the 'Gotik mahalle' route?

The 'Gotik mahalle' route takes you past Barcelona Cathedral, Palau de la Música Catalana, Plaça Reial, and Mercat de la Boqueria. It covers 2.304 km and takes approximately 28 minutes and 45 seconds to walk.

## Are there any restroom or water points along the 'Gotik mahalle' route?

Unfortunately, no restroom or drinking water points have been recorded along the 'Gotik mahalle' route.

## What does the 'Passeig de Gracia' route include?

This route features Casa Batlló, Casa Milà, Plaça de Catalunya, and Casa Amatller. The total distance is 2.723 km, and the walk takes about 34 minutes and 17 seconds. A Starbucks cafe is listed as a water and restroom point.

## Where does the 'Sahil ve Ciutadella' route go?

The 'Sahil ve Ciutadella' route passes by Arc de Triomf, Parc de la Ciutadella, Port Olímpic, and Barceloneta Plajı. It’s the longest route, spanning 3.976 km and taking roughly 49 minutes and 16 seconds. Public restrooms are identified along the route.

## Does Laloo verify opening hours or accessibility of the places on the routes?

Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility of the locations featured on its walking routes.
