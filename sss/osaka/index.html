# Walking in Osaka: FAQ

## What is Laloo?

Laloo is a website offering walking routes in Osaka. It helps you explore the city on foot, highlighting locations of restrooms and drinking water points near the routes.

## How many walking routes are available in Osaka on Laloo?

Currently, there are two walking routes available in Osaka through Laloo.

## Can you tell me about the 'Osaka Kalesi ve cevresi' route?

This route, translated as 'Osaka Castle and Surroundings,' covers a distance of 3.468 kilometers and takes approximately 44 minutes and 41 seconds to complete. It includes stops at Osaka Castle, Osaka Castle Park, Osaka Museum of History, and the Osakajo-koen Station area.

## What does the 'Umeda ve Kita' route involve?

The 'Umeda ve Kita' route is 3.769 kilometers long and takes around 46 minutes and 18 seconds to walk. It passes through Osaka Station, Grand Front Osaka, Umeda Sky Building, and Nakanoshima Park.

## Are restroom and drinking water locations indicated on the routes?

Yes, Laloo routes show locations of restrooms and drinking water points near the route. However, currently, none have been recorded for the Osaka routes.

## Does Laloo provide information about the opening hours or fees for the places visited on the routes?

No, Laloo does not verify the opening hours, fees, or accessibility of the stops featured on its routes.
