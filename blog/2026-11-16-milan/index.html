# Exploring Milan on Foot

Milan offers a fantastic way to experience the city – by walking! There are several well-defined routes to help you discover different areas at your own pace. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and adjust your speed to suit your preferences. Taking short breaks is always a good idea too. Let’s take a look at two of these routes.

## Route 1: From Cathedral to Castle

This walk, themed “Cathedral and Castle,” takes you on a journey connecting two significant landmarks. The route begins at the Duomo di Milano, a cathedral, and leads you through the city to the Castello Sforzesco, a castle. Along the way, you'll pass through Piazza della Scala, a square, and Via Dante, another square. The entire route covers a distance of approximately 2.042 kilometers and is expected to take around 25 minutes and 28 seconds to complete. It’s a relatively short walk, perfect for a quick overview of the central area. Please note that there are no recorded restroom or water points directly along this particular route.

## Route 2: Castle and Park

For a slightly longer exploration, consider the “Castle and Park” route. This walk starts at the Castello Sforzesco, a castle, and leads you through Parco Sempione, a park. The journey continues to Arco della Pace, a monument, before concluding at the Basilica di Sant'Ambrogio, a cathedral. The total distance of this route is 3.179 kilometers, and it is estimated to take approximately 38 minutes and 33 seconds to walk. This route offers a chance to experience a mix of urban and green spaces. A cafe, La Rocchetta, provides restroom and water facilities nearby.

Enjoy your walking adventures in Milan!
