# Exploring Taipei on Foot

Taipei offers a fantastic way to experience the city – on foot! Several established walking routes provide structured explorations of different areas. These routes are designed to be manageable and give you a good feel for the urban landscape. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, pace yourself, and stay hydrated – especially important in a warm climate. Look out for opportunities to take a break and refresh with water along the way.

## Route 1: Ximending and Memorials

This route focuses on a combination of significant monuments and a bustling public space. It begins at the Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall, a monument, and moves on to 228 Peace Memorial Park, a park. The walk then continues to the Presidential Office Building, another monument, before concluding at Ximending, a square. The total distance of this route is just over 3 kilometers, and it should take approximately 38 minutes and 52 seconds to complete. Drinking water is available for those who need it.

## Route 2: 228 Park and Taipei Station

This route offers a slightly shorter experience, centred around a park and a major transport hub. It starts at 228 Peace Memorial Park, a park, and proceeds to the National Taiwan Museum, a museum. The route then takes you to Taipei Main Station, a monument, and finishes at Zhongshan Hall, another monument. This walk covers a distance of around 2.5 kilometers and is expected to take about 34 minutes and 14 seconds. A 7-Eleven, a cafe, provides a convenient location for refreshments.

Enjoy your walking adventures in Taipei!
