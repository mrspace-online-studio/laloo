Exploring Seoul on Foot

Seoul offers a fantastic way to experience the city – by walking! Several established routes provide structured explorations, allowing you to discover different areas at a comfortable pace. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks as needed.

Route 1: Gyeongbokgung and Insadong

This route explores a section of Seoul blending historic and cultural highlights. It begins at a palace, continues through a traditional neighborhood, then visits a temple, before concluding on a street. The total distance covered is 3.294 kilometers, and the walk is estimated to take around 40 minutes and 20 seconds. There are no recorded restroom or water points directly along this particular route, so planning ahead is recommended.

The stops on this route are:

  • Gyeongbokgung Palace (palace)
  • Bukchon Hanok Village (bolge)
  • Jogyesa Temple (temple)
  • Insadong (street)

Route 2: City Hall and Myeongdong

This walking route focuses on a different part of the city, connecting a government building, a palace, a cathedral, and a traditional village. The route spans 3.512 kilometers and is expected to take approximately 49 minutes and 35 seconds to complete. Like the previous route, no restroom or water points were recorded along the path.

The stops on this route are:

  • Seoul City Hall (bina)
  • Deoksugung Palace (palace)
  • Myeongdong Cathedral (katedral)
  • Namsangol Hanok Village (bolge)

Enjoy your exploration of Seoul!
