# Exploring Madrid on Foot

Madrid is a fantastic city to discover, and what better way to do it than by walking? Here are two suggested walking routes, perfect for getting a feel for different areas and enjoying the city at a relaxed pace. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks when needed!

## Route 1: The Artistic Triangle

This route explores a selection of cultural highlights, connecting museums and prominent public spaces. You’ll begin at a museum, then proceed to another museum. Following this, the route leads to a square, and concludes at another square. The total distance is 2.68 kilometers, and the walk should take approximately 33 minutes and 52 seconds. A cafe, La Venganza de Malinche, offers restroom and water facilities nearby.

## Route 2: Palace and Gran Via

This route focuses on the historical and central areas of the city. It starts at a palace, continues to a square, then moves on to a monument, and finishes at a square. The distance is slightly shorter at 2.146 kilometers, with an estimated walking time of 30 minutes and 19 seconds. Please note that there are no recorded restroom or water facilities specifically noted close to this route.
