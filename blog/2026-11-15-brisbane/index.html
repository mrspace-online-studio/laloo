# Exploring Brisbane on Foot

Brisbane offers some lovely opportunities to discover the city through walking. Here are two established routes you can follow, each offering a different perspective on the area. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, maintain a pace that suits you, and take breaks when needed. Staying hydrated is also important, especially on warmer days.

## Route 1: South Bank and the River

This route explores the area of South Bank and its riverside surroundings. It’s a pleasant walk covering a distance of just over 2.494 kilometers, which should take approximately 30 minutes and 54 seconds to complete.

The route includes the following stops:

* Queensland Performing Arts Centre (monument)
* South Bank Parklands (park)
* Queensland Museum (museum)
* Victoria Bridge (bridge)
* Brisbane City Hall (monument)

Please note that there are no recorded restroom or water points directly along this route, so planning ahead is advisable.

## Route 2: Botanic Gardens and the City Centre

For a slightly longer walk, consider this route which focuses on the city centre and adjacent gardens. This one stretches for 3.182 kilometers and is estimated to take about 41 minutes and 36 seconds.

The route’s stops are:

* City Botanic Gardens (park)
* Parliament House (monument)
* Queen Street Mall (square)
* ANZAC Square (square)
* Roma Street Parkland (park)

As with the previous route, there are no recorded restroom or water points along the way.
