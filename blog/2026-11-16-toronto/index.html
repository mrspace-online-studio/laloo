# Exploring Toronto on Foot

Toronto offers a fantastic way to see the city – by walking! Discovering different neighbourhoods and landmarks at your own pace is a truly rewarding experience. Here are two walking routes to get you started, designed to showcase distinct areas of the city. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks as needed.

## Route 1: City Centre and Universities

This route, themed “City Centre and Universities,” takes you through the heart of the city and towards areas of learning. You’ll pass several important landmarks.

The walk begins at Toronto City Hall, then proceeds to Osgoode Hall. Next, you’ll head to the Art Gallery of Ontario, followed by Queen's Park. This route covers a distance of 2.731 kilometers and is estimated to take approximately 33 minutes and 55 seconds to complete. Please note that there are no recorded restroom or water points directly along this route, so plan accordingly.

## Route 2: Waterfront and City Centre

For a different perspective, try this route, themed "Waterfront and City Centre." It focuses on the area around the harbour and the central business district.

The walk starts at Union Station, then moves on to the CN Tower. You'll then visit Harbourfront Centre and conclude at St Lawrence Market. This route is a little longer, measuring 3.335 kilometers, and is expected to take around 39 minutes and 45 seconds. As with the previous route, there are no recorded restroom or water points along the way.
