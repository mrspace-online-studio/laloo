Exploring Lyon on Foot: Discover the City's Heart

Lyon offers a fantastic way to experience its vibrant atmosphere – by walking! The city has developed a series of walking routes to help you discover different areas, and we're excited to share a couple of them with you. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, pace yourself, and stay hydrated – especially on warmer days.

Route 1: The Heart of the Peninsula

This route, themed "The Heart of the Peninsula," takes you on a journey through a central area. It begins at Place Bellecour, then moves to Place des Jacobins. The walk continues to Hôtel de Ville de Lyon, before concluding at Place des Terreaux.

The total distance covered is just over 2 kilometers. This route can be completed in approximately 25 minutes and 38 seconds. While you’re enjoying the walk, be aware that there are no recorded restroom or water points directly along the route.

Route 2: Vieux Lyon Renaissance

This route explores the historic area of Vieux Lyon. The stops are: Cathédrale Saint-Jean (cathedral), Place du Change (square), Hôtel de Bullion (monument), and Maison du Gouverneur (house). The route covers a distance of 1.431 km, and takes around 21 minutes and 2 seconds to walk. Restroom/water points near the route: none recorded.

Route 3: Les Murs de Lyon

This route, called "The Walls of Lyon", is a little longer. You'll start at Place Saint-Louis (square) and then proceed to the Croix-Rousse (district). Next, the route passes by the Institut Médico-Éducatif (institute) before ending at the Parc des Hauteurs (park). The total distance is 3.657 km, and the estimated walking time is 46 minutes and 27 seconds. As with the other routes, there are no recorded restroom or water points on this particular path.
