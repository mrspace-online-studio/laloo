Singapore on Foot: Exploring the City with Laloo Routes

Singapore offers a fantastic opportunity to discover its unique character at a relaxed pace. The Laloo walking routes provide a structured way to experience different areas, and we're highlighting a few to get you started. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, maintain a sustainable walking speed, and stay hydrated – especially in Singapore’s climate!

Marina & River Route

This route, themed "Marina and River," is a great introduction to the city’s waterfront. The journey begins at Merlion Park, then continues to the Esplanade. Next, you’ll cross the Helix Bridge before arriving at Gardens by the Bay. The entire route covers a distance of 2.528 kilometers and is estimated to take approximately 33 minutes and 30 seconds to complete. It's worth noting that there are no recorded restroom or water points directly along this particular route, so planning ahead is recommended.
