Exploring Prague on Foot

Prague is a fantastic city to discover at your own pace, and walking is a brilliant way to experience its different neighbourhoods. We've put together some suggested routes to help you get started. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks when you need them!

Route 1: Historic Heart & Public Spaces

This route takes you through the central area, focusing on significant public areas and a notable building. The journey covers 2.46 kilometers and should take approximately 29 minutes and 47 seconds to complete.

The route begins at Wenceslas Square, then moves on to a historical building, followed by Old Town Square. The final destination is Charles Bridge.

Route 2: Castle District & Gardens

For a different perspective, this route explores the Castle District and surrounding areas. The total distance is 2.606 kilometers, with an estimated walking time of 31 minutes and 56 seconds.

You'll start at Prague Castle, then proceed to St. Vitus Cathedral. The route continues to a monument, and concludes with a visit to Wallenstein Garden.

Enjoy your walks!
