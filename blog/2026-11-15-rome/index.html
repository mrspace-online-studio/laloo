# Exploring Rome on Foot

Rome is a fantastic city to discover on foot, offering a chance to experience its atmosphere at a relaxed pace. Here are three walking routes, each with a different focus, to help you make the most of your visit. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks as needed!

## Ancient Rome

This route explores some of the city’s most significant historical locations, focusing on the ancient world. Your journey begins at a monument, followed by an ancient site. The route then continues to another ancient site and concludes at a large open space. The total distance covered is approximately 2.444 kilometers, and the walk should take around 30 minutes and 47 seconds. You'll find opportunities to access drinking water along the way.

## Squares and Fountains

This route takes you to some of the city’s most celebrated public spaces and water features. The walk begins at a square, then moves on to a monument. Next, you’ll visit a fountain, and the route ends at another square. This route is shorter, covering 2.069 kilometers and taking roughly 26 minutes and 37 seconds. Please note that there are no recorded locations to access restrooms or water along this particular route.

## River and Square

This route combines a riverside experience with visits to notable squares. The walk starts at a square, then progresses to a bridge. You’ll then visit a castle before returning to a square. This is the longest of the three routes, stretching 3.209 kilometers and taking approximately 40 minutes and 31 seconds. A cafe offering refreshments and water is available at Monte d'Oro.

Enjoy your walking adventures in Rome!
