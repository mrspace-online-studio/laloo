Exploring Osaka on Foot

Osaka is a fantastic city to discover, and one of the best ways to experience it is simply by walking! There are some well-planned walking routes available to help you explore different areas. Here's a look at two of them, offering a chance to see a variety of locations at a relaxed pace. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks as needed.

Route 1: Osaka Castle and its Surroundings

This route focuses on the area around Osaka Castle, giving you a chance to see several key landmarks. The journey begins at Osaka Castle, continues through Osaka Castle Park, then on to the Osaka Museum of History. The final stop on this route is the Osakajo-koen Station area.

The total distance covered is 3.468 kilometers, and the estimated walking time is approximately 44 minutes and 41 seconds. Keep in mind that this is an estimate, and your own pace may vary. There are no recorded restroom or water points directly along this route, so it's important to plan accordingly.

Route 2: Umeda and Kita

This route takes you through the Umeda and Kita districts, showcasing a different side of Osaka. You'll start at Osaka Station, then proceed to Grand Front Osaka, followed by the Umeda Sky Building. The route concludes at Nakanoshima Park.

This route is slightly longer, measuring 3.769 kilometers. The estimated walking time is about 46 minutes and 18 seconds. As with the previous route, there are no recorded restroom or water points along the way, so be sure to bring your own refreshments and plan for breaks.

Enjoy your walking adventures in Osaka!
