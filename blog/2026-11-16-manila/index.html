# Exploring Manila on Foot

Manila offers a fantastic way to experience the city – by walking! There are some well-defined routes that showcase different areas and provide a structured way to discover more. Here’s a look at two options to get you started. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, maintain a pace that suits you, and take breaks when needed. Staying hydrated is also essential, so be sure to have water available.

## Route 1: Ermita and Rizal

This walking route focuses on the Ermita and Rizal areas of the city. It's a pleasant journey that takes you past several key locations.

The route begins at Rizal Park, a large park, and then continues to the National Museum of Fine Arts, a museum. From there, you'll move on to Manila City Hall, a monument, before concluding at Intramuros, a castle. The entire walk covers a distance of 2.211 kilometers and should take approximately 30 minutes and 38 seconds to complete. Unfortunately, there are no recorded restroom or water facilities directly along this route, so plan accordingly.

## Route 2: Rizal Park and Surroundings

This second route centers around Rizal Park and the surrounding area, offering a slightly different perspective.

You’ll start, as before, at Rizal Park, a park, and then proceed to the National Museum of Fine Arts, a museum. The route continues to the National Museum of Anthropology, also a museum, before ending at Paco Park, another park. This walk is a little longer, stretching 2.27 kilometers, and is estimated to take around 30 minutes and 5 seconds. As with the previous route, there are no recorded restroom or water points along the way.
