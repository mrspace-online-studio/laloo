Exploring Lisbon on Foot

Lisbon is a fantastic city to explore, and what better way to discover its charm than by walking? We've put together some guided walking routes to help you experience different areas and enjoy the city at your own pace. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks as needed!

Route 1: Alfama and Viewpoints

This route focuses on the Alfama district and its stunning viewpoints. It begins at Lisbon Cathedral and leads you through the historic streets to Miradouro de Santa Luzia, a pleasant park. Continuing on, you’ll reach Castelo de Sao Jorge, a castle, before finishing at Miradouro da Graca, another park offering panoramic perspectives.

The entire route covers a distance of 2.251 kilometers and takes approximately 32 minutes and 5 seconds to complete. Please note that there are no recorded restroom or water points directly along this particular walking path, so planning accordingly is advisable.

Route 2 - theme: "Mouraria e Graça". Stops in order: Martim Moniz (square), Igreja de São Cristóvão (church), Miradouro Senhora do Monte (park), Largo da Graça (square). Distance: 1.368 km, about 19 dk 38 sn walking. Restroom/water points near the route: none recorded.

Next up is a journey through the Mouraria and Graça neighborhoods. This route starts at Martim Moniz, a square, and proceeds to Igreja de São Cristóvão, a church. You’ll then ascend to Miradouro Senhora do Monte, a park providing elevated views, before concluding at Largo da Graça, a square.

This shorter route spans 1.368 kilometers and is estimated to take around 19 minutes and 38 seconds. As with the previous route, there are no recorded locations for restrooms or drinking water along the way.

Route 3 - theme: "Graça e São Vicente". Stops in order: Miradouro da Graça (park), Igreja de São Vicente de Fora (church), Campo de Ourique (square). Distance: 2.332 km, about 33 dk 59 sn walking. Restroom/water points near the route: none recorded.

Our final route takes you from the Graça district towards São Vicente. It begins at Miradouro da Graça, a park, and moves on to Igreja de São Vicente de Fora, a church. The route then finishes at Campo de Ourique, a square.

This route is slightly longer, covering 2.332 kilometers and requiring approximately 33 minutes and 59 seconds to complete. Remember to bring water and take breaks as needed. There are no recorded restroom or water points along this route.
