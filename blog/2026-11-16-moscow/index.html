Exploring Moscow on Foot

Moscow offers fantastic opportunities for those who enjoy discovering a city by walking. Here are a couple of routes to get you started, designed to showcase different areas and experiences. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, pace yourself, and stay hydrated – especially on longer walks!

Route 1: Heart of the City

This route focuses on the central area, exploring key landmarks. The theme is “Kizil Meydan ve Kremlin,” which translates to “Red Square and the Kremlin.”

The walk begins at Okhotny Ryad, a square, and continues to the Alexander Garden, a park. From there, you’ll move on to Red Square, another square, before reaching Saint Basil's Cathedral, a cathedral. The final stop on this route is GUM, a monument.

The total distance covered is 2.809 kilometers, and the estimated walking time is approximately 34 minutes and 16 seconds. It’s a relatively short route, perfect for a morning or afternoon exploration. Please note that no restroom or water facilities were recorded as being directly accessible along this particular route.
