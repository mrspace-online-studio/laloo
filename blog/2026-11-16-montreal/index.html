# Montreal on Foot: Exploring the City's Walking Routes

Montreal is a fantastic city to explore, and what better way to discover it than on foot? The Laloo walking routes offer a structured way to experience different areas, and we’re excited to share a couple of them with you. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, adjust your pace to enjoy the journey, and stay hydrated!

## Route 1: Old Montreal and the Waterfront

This route, themed "Old Montreal and the Harbor," takes you through a historic area and along the waterfront. The walk begins at a basilica, then continues to a square. Next, you’ll head to a square located at the Old Port of Montreal, followed by a square in Chinatown Montreal. The total distance is 2.435 kilometers, and the estimated walking time is approximately 29 minutes and 28 seconds. There are no restroom or water points specifically noted along this route, so be sure to plan accordingly.

## Route 2: "Le Plateau Mont-Royal et ses environs" (The Plateau Mont-Royal and its surroundings). Stops in order: Parc La Fontaine (park), Mont-Royal Avenue (street), Saint-Denis Street (street), Beaver Hall Library (library). Distance: 3.115 km, about 40 dk 10 sn walking. Restroom/water points near the route: none recorded.

Route 2, themed "The Plateau Mont-Royal and its surroundings," offers a slightly longer exploration of a vibrant neighborhood. This route starts at a park, then moves onto a street. You’ll then walk along another street, before finishing at a library. The distance covered is 3.115 kilometers, with an estimated walking time of 40 minutes and 10 seconds. As with the previous route, there are no recorded restroom or water points nearby, so it's important to come prepared.

We hope these routes inspire you to get out and explore Montreal! Remember to take breaks when needed and enjoy the journey.
