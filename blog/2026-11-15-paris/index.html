# Exploring Paris on Foot

Paris is a wonderful city to discover, and what better way to experience it than by walking? These curated routes offer a chance to see several key locations at a comfortable pace. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks when needed!

## Historic Heart and River

This route focuses on the historic center and the river, covering a distance of just over 2 kilometers. The walk itself should take approximately 28 minutes and 24 seconds. You’ll pass by Notre-Dame de Paris, Sainte-Chapelle, the Conciergerie, and the Louvre Museum. Public restrooms are accessible nearby should you require them.

## Louvre and Palais Royal

For those interested in art and grand architecture, this route explores the area around the Louvre and Palais Royal. The distance is a little longer, at nearly 2.8 kilometers, and the estimated walking time is around 35 minutes and 18 seconds. You’ll visit the Louvre Museum, Palais Royal, Opéra Garnier, and the Jardin des Tuileries. A cafe, Tiger Sugar, provides restroom and water facilities along the way.

## Montmartre

The final route takes you to the charming neighborhood of Montmartre. This walk is just over 2 kilometers and should take roughly 25 minutes and 42 seconds. You'll pass the Sacré-Cœur Basilica, Place du Tertre, the Moulin Rouge, and the Wall of Love. Please note that no restroom or water facilities have been recorded along this particular route.

Enjoy your Parisian explorations!
