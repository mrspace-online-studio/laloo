Exploring Valencia on Foot

Valencia is a wonderful city to discover, and what better way to experience it than by walking? We’ve put together a selection of walking routes to help you get acquainted with the area. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, pace yourself, and stay hydrated – especially during warmer months!

Route 1: Park and Art

This route focuses on the green spaces and artistic offerings of Valencia. It begins at Torres de Serranos, a monument, and leads you through Jardin del Turia, a park. The walk then takes you past Museo de Bellas Artes de Valencia, a museum, before concluding at Puente del Real, a bridge.

The total distance of this route is 3.401 kilometers, and it should take approximately 43 minutes and 16 seconds to complete at a moderate pace. It's a good option for a relaxed morning or afternoon stroll. Please note that there are no recorded restroom or water points directly along this particular route, so planning ahead is recommended.

Route 2 - theme: "Valencia'nin tarihi sokaklari" (translate: "Valencia's historic streets"). Stops in order: Plaza del Mercat (square), Estación del Norte (train station), Mercado Colón (market), Catedral de Valencia (cathedral). Distance: 2.087 km, about 28 dk 58 sn walking. Restroom/water points near the route: none recorded.

Route 2: Historic Streets

Journey through Valencia’s past with this route, which showcases some of the city's significant landmarks. It starts at Plaza del Mercat, a square, and continues to Estación del Norte, a train station. The walk then passes by Mercado Colón, a market, before finishing at Catedral de Valencia, a cathedral.

This route covers 2.087 kilometers and should take around 28 minutes and 58 seconds to complete. It’s a shorter option, perfect for those wanting a taste of the city's historical heart. As with the previous route, there are no recorded restroom or water points along the way, so be sure to prepare accordingly.

Route 3 - theme: “Valencia'nin deniz kenari” (translate: "Valencia's seaside"). Stops in order: Port Saplaya (harbour), El Saler (nature reserve), Malvarrosa Beach (beach), Parque de la Tradición Valenciana (park). Distance: 6.958 km, about 1 saat 26 dk 59 sn walking. Restroom/water points near the route: none recorded.

Route 3: Seaside Valencia

Experience the coastal beauty of Valencia with this longer route. It begins at Port Saplaya, a harbour, and leads you to El Saler, a nature reserve. The walk continues to Malvarrosa Beach, a beach, and concludes at Parque de la Tradición Valenciana, a park.

This route is the longest of the three, spanning 6.958 kilometers and taking approximately 1 hour and 26 minutes and 59 seconds to walk. It’s ideal for those who enjoy a longer, more immersive experience. Remember to bring plenty of water and take breaks as needed. There are no recorded restroom or water points along this route.
