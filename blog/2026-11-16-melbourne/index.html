# Exploring Melbourne on Foot: Two Walking Routes

Melbourne is a fantastic city to explore, and what better way to discover it than by walking? Here are two suggested routes, perfect for getting a feel for different areas and enjoying the urban landscape. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, maintain a pace that suits you, and take breaks when needed. Staying hydrated is also key, so be sure to utilize the available water points along the way.

## Federation and Degraves

This route, themed "Federation and Degraves," offers a journey through the heart of the city. It begins at Flinders Street Station, a notable monument. From there, the walk continues to Federation Square, a large open square. Next, you’ll pass St Paul's Cathedral, a cathedral, before concluding at Degraves Street, another square.

The entire route covers a distance of 3.913 kilometers and is estimated to take approximately 51 minutes and 41 seconds to complete. Drinking water is readily accessible along the route for refreshment.

## Docklands and Flagstaff

The "Docklands and Flagstaff" route provides a different perspective, venturing towards the waterfront. It starts at Queen Victoria Market, a bustling square, then moves on to Flagstaff Gardens, a park. The route then passes Southern Cross Station, another monument, before finishing at Docklands, a square.

This route is slightly shorter, spanning 3.294 kilometers and taking around 41 minutes and 15 seconds to walk. You’ll find drinking water available to help you stay hydrated throughout your exploration.

Happy walking!
