# Exploring Zurich on Foot: Two Scenic Walking Routes

Zurich offers a wonderful way to experience the city – by foot! Discovering the area through walking allows you to appreciate its unique character and enjoy the surroundings at your own pace. Here are two prepared walking routes to help you get started. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks as needed.

## Route 1: Old Town & Lake

This route, themed “Old Town and Lake,” takes you through a charming section of Zurich. It begins at Zürich HB, a monument, and then proceeds to Bahnhofstrasse, a square. The walk continues to Bürkliplatz, another square, before concluding at Grossmünster, a cathedral.

The total distance for this route is 2.11 kilometers, and it’s estimated to take approximately 26 minutes and 30 seconds to complete. While you’re out enjoying the walk, please note that there are no recorded restroom or water points directly along the route.

## Route 2: Limmat River & City Hall

The second route, “Limmat River and City Hall,” provides a slightly longer exploration. It starts at Zurich Main Station, a monument, and moves to Bahnhofstrasse, a square. From there, you'll walk to Lindenhof, a park, and then finish at Rathaus Zürich, a monument.

This route covers a greater distance of 2.676 kilometers and is expected to take around 33 minutes and 2 seconds. Conveniently, there is a drinking water point available for refreshment along the way.

Enjoy your walking adventures in Zurich!
