Exploring Budapest on Foot

Budapest is a fantastic city to discover at a leisurely pace, and walking is a wonderful way to experience it. Here are two suggested routes, designed to showcase different aspects of the city. Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks as needed!

Route 1: Parliament and Basilica

This route focuses on some of the prominent landmarks on the Pest side of the city. You'll begin at the Hungarian Parliament, then move on to Kossuth Lajos tér, a public square. The route continues to St. Stephen's Basilica, and concludes with a walk across the Széchenyi Chain Bridge.

The total distance of this walk is 2.517 kilometers, and it should take approximately 30 minutes and 17 seconds to complete. It’s a good idea to factor in extra time for pauses and to appreciate your surroundings. Please note that there are no recorded restroom or water points directly along this route, so plan accordingly.

Route 2: Castle and River

This second route takes you to the Buda side, offering a different perspective of the city. The journey starts at Buda Castle, a significant palace. Next, you’ll visit Matthias Church, followed by the Fisherman’s Bastion, a monument. The route finishes with a crossing of the Chain Bridge.

This walk covers a distance of 2.042 kilometers and is estimated to take around 24 minutes and 48 seconds. As with the previous route, there are no recorded facilities for restrooms or water along the way.

Enjoy your walks in Budapest!
